# Lessons from 2026-09-21_123655_pod-the-wise-mothman-edgar-markov-tidus-_0031, 2026-09-21_123657_pod-miirym-fire-lord-zuko-yuriko-fire-lo_0032, 2026-09-21_124814_pod-fire-lord-zuko-terra-hearthhull-zaxa_0033, 2026-09-21_132410_pod-mr-house-fire-lord-zuko-choco-feloth_0034

- **opening_hand** Mulligan a seven with two lands unless it also has a mana rock and at least two plays castable off those three sources. Even with the rock, play the rock on turn 2 and expect to sit on three sources for several turns: this list has 36 lands and its payoffs cost 4 to 6. _Evidence: Game 0034: it kept Mountain + Marsh Flats + Talisman of Conviction and missed land drops on rounds 3, 5 and 6, never getting past 3 lands. Only Sol Ring and a foretold Sozin's Comet rescued it. Game 0032's two-lander worked only because Legion Leadership was a third land._
- **mana** Hold X spells and pump for combat, and plan every turn you attack with Zuko around a way to spend the firebending red, because it empties at end of combat. Good ways: flash in Embercleave (it costs {1} less per attacker), Electrodominance with a big X (which also casts a free spell from hand), Invoke Calamity, The Last Agni Kai. _Evidence: Game 0032 round 4: firebending red paid for Embercleave, making Zuko a 6/8 double striker for 12. Game 0034 round 6: a 26-red pool cast Electrodominance X=22, which knocked Choco out of the game, and its free cast put Ozai onto the battlefield. Game 0033 round 5: 6 firebending red emptied unused because the hand had nothing to spend it on._
- **sequencing** Your biggest turn is foretelling Sozin's Comet with spare mana, then casting it from exile for {2}{R} with Isshin on the battlefield. The cast from exile puts a counter on each creature through Zuko, and Isshin makes every firebending trigger happen twice. Keep an X spell in hand for that attack. _Evidence: Game 0034: on round 5 it foretold the Comet off Sol Ring. On round 6 it cast the Comet from exile and attacked with Zuko, Daydreamer and Isshin. The doubled firebending triggers gave it 26 red, enough for Electrodominance X=22 on Choco at 22 life._
- **how_a_card_works** Norin the Wary is a Zuko engine. Every time any player casts a spell or a creature attacks, Norin is exiled, and it returns at the next end step. That counts as a permanent entering from exile, so if Zuko is on the battlefield, every creature you control gets a +1/+1 counter. Cast Norin before Zuko, and resolve the Zuko trigger on every return. _Evidence: Game 0032 round 3: Zuko was cast, Norin was exiled by that cast and returned at the end step, and Zuko became 3/5 immediately. By round 4 Zuko had 3 counters without any other exile card._
- **how_a_card_works** While Zuko is on the battlefield, always take Goliath Daydreamer's free recast from exile, even when it does nothing (Electrodominance with X=0). Casting a spell from exile puts a +1/+1 counter on each of your creatures. Daydreamer only re-exiles spells cast from hand, so the recast copy goes to the graveyard. _Evidence: Game 0033: on round 3 the pilot declined the X=0 recast because Zuko was not out. On round 4, with Zuko out, the same X=0 Electrodominance gave every creature a counter, and on round 5 the Hex Magic recast did too. Game 0034 round 6: the X=0 Electrodominance recast added another counter._
- **interaction** Ozai has indestructible while you hold six or more unspent mana, and he turns mana you would lose into red. When removal targets him, tap lands in response to reach six floating, and do not dump the pool below six on your own turn. _Evidence: Game 0033 round 7: Hearthhull cast Putrefy on Ozai while the pool held 5. The pilot tapped a Mountain in response to reach 6, and Ozai survived. The auditor confirmed the pool should be kept (14 R) from turn to turn._
