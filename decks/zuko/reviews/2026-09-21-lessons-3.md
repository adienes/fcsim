# Lessons from 2026-09-21_123655_pod-the-wise-mothman-edgar-markov-tidus-_0031, 2026-09-21_123657_pod-miirym-fire-lord-zuko-yuriko-fire-lo_0032, 2026-09-21_124814_pod-fire-lord-zuko-terra-hearthhull-zaxa_0033, 2026-09-21_132410_pod-mr-house-fire-lord-zuko-choco-feloth_0034

- **how_a_card_works** When Fire Lord Zuko is bounced to your hand, recasting him from hand costs only {R}{W}{B}. Commander tax applies only to casts from the command zone. Plan the turn's mana with the correct cost; don't budget 5 for him. _Evidence: Game 0031: Summon: Valefor bounced Zuko to hand on turn 17. On turn 18 the pilot wrote 'That covers Zuko (5 with commander tax) or Etali (6), but not both.' On turn 22 it paid tax from hand, tapping Arcane Signet and Shinka, and then had to untap them to fix its own overpayment._
- **sequencing** When one card is in hand and another sits in exile with a play window that closes this turn, play the exiled one first unless the hand card is clearly more urgent. The card in hand will still be there next turn; the exiled one is gone forever. _Evidence: Game 0031, round 6 (turn 22): Light Up the Stage had exiled Etali, Primal Storm and Dragonskull Summit, playable only until the end of that turn. The pilot spent the turn recasting Zuko and Party Thrasher from hand, which would still have been castable next turn, and noted 'Etali ... I didn't have the mana, so I lost it.'_
- **sequencing** Cast Norin the Wary on turn 2, before Zuko, and leave him out. Every spell anyone casts exiles him, and he returns at the next end step, which triggers Zuko's counter for your whole team nearly every turn at no cost. _Evidence: Game 0032: Norin came down on turn 7 (round 2) and Zuko on turn 11. Norin blinked on turns 11, 12 and 13, so by turn 15 Zuko was 5 power with 3 counters without casting anything from exile. His firebending 5 then paid for a flashed-in Embercleave, and he attacked as a 6/8 double striker for 12._
- **sequencing** Foretell Delayed Blast Fireball as soon as you have a spare {2}, then cast it from exile the next turn, letting Party Thrasher's convoke pay part of the {4}{R}{R}. Five damage to every opponent and each of their creatures is this deck's reset button against go-wide boards. Afterwards, Light Up the Stage costs only its {R} spectacle cost. _Evidence: Game 0031: Fireball was foretold on turn 14 alongside Thrasher. On turn 18 it was cast from exile with Thrasher tapped for convoke. It killed all of Edgar's vampires, Hancock, O'aka, Kami and Gyre Sage, and hit each opponent for 5. Light Up the Stage followed for {R} off Shinka._
- **how_a_card_works** The free cast from Goliath Daydreamer's attack trigger sets X to 0, so a recast Electrodominance deals no damage. It is still a spell cast from exile, which triggers Zuko. Decline the free cast while Zuko is off the battlefield, then take it once he is out, purely for the team-wide counter. _Evidence: Game 0033: on turn 10 the pilot declined the free Electrodominance ('X=0 ... keeping it in exile for a later attack with Zuko out'). On turn 14, with Zuko in play, it cast it free for X=0, and Zuko put a counter on every creature. Game 0034, turn 23: the same X=0 recast was used again for a Zuko trigger._
- **commander_timing** On the Comet turn, cast the foretold Sozin's Comet for {2}{R} before combat with Zuko and Isshin out, and attack with everything. Isshin doubles each firebending trigger. Then pour the pool into Electrodominance at an opponent you can kill outright, and use its free cast for Ozai so the leftover mana stays in your pool. _Evidence: Game 0034, round 6 (turn 23): the Comet was foretold on turn 19 with Sol Ring mana and cast from exile on turn 23. Three attackers with doubled firebending produced 26 red. Electrodominance at X=22 took Choco from exactly 22 to 0 and cast Ozai for free. Then Last Agni Kai fights killed Mr. House's commander and Iron Mastiff._
- **interaction** When removal targets Zuko during combat, respond by casting any spell you can from exile (for example Great Train Heist with the floating firebending mana). Zuko's counter trigger resolves before he leaves. With Ozai out, answer destroy removal by tapping lands until you have 6 unspent mana, which makes him indestructible. _Evidence: Game 0033, turn 22: Terra cast Path to Exile on attacking Zuko. The pilot cast Great Train Heist from exile in response with all three modes, and the team got counters before Zuko left. On turn 25, Hearthhull cast Putrefy on Ozai. The pilot tapped a Mountain to bring the pool to 6, and Ozai survived._
- **how_a_card_works** A permanent with a stun counter does not untap in your untap step; one stun counter comes off instead. Once a Saga like Summon: Valefor has stunned your creatures, plan your turn without them: don't declare them as attackers or count them as blockers. _Evidence: Game 0031, turn 28: Zuko and Runehorn Hellkite each had 2 stun counters from Summon: Valefor. Hellkite was declared attacking Elspeth anyway with Pip-Boy equipped. The auditor undid the attack and Elspeth came back at 7 loyalty, so the pilot lost both the equip mana and the planeswalker kill._
