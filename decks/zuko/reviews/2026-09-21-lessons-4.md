# Lessons from 2026-09-21_092534_pod-esika-tidus-fire-lord-zuko-kilo_0012, 2026-09-21_104400_pod-hearthhull-kuja-omnath-fire-lord-zuk_0021, 2026-09-21_105026_pod-obeka-fire-lord-zuko-ms-bumbleflower_0023

- **sequencing** Cast haste creatures such as Laelia, the Blade Reforged in main phase 1, before you pass priority with an empty stack. Passing on an empty stack moves you to combat, and a Laelia cast after combat loses her attack trigger for the turn: no exiled card and no counter. _Evidence: Game 0021 (Kuja pod), round 4 (table turn 13): the pilot cast Talisman of Conviction, then passed by mistake and ended up in combat. It attacked with Zuko alone for 2 and cast Laelia in main 2. Its note: 'I meant to cast Laelia before combat so she could attack, but an extra pass moved me out of main phase 1 too early.' Contrast game 0012, round 3 (turn 11): Laelia went down in main 1, attacked the same turn, exiled Arid Mesa and was played from exile._
- **threat_assessment** When an opponent controls Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider, the single +1/+1 counters from Zuko and Laelia and the quest counters on Firebender Ascension all round down to 0. Stop building toward counters and switch to the damage finish: attack, cast Storm King's Thunder with the firebending mana at end of combat, then cast Chandra's Ignition on your biggest creature in main 2. Keep the red with Leyline Tyrant, and count every copy against each opponent's life before you commit. _Evidence: Game 0012: from round 5 (turn 19) Vorinclex halved every Zuko, Laelia and Ascension counter to 0, as the pilot noted. In round 8 (turn 30) it attacked, paid for Storm King's Thunder X=3 with 2 firebending R, 1 R kept by Tyrant, Plains, Swamp and Talisman, then cast Chandra's Ignition on 5-power Laelia. Four Ignitions dealt 20 to each opponent, which killed Tidus (12) and Esika (19) together._
- **combat** With Avatar Roku out, attack and let his six R plus Zuko's firebending pay for everything during combat. After blocks are declared, flash in Embercleave (1 cheaper per attacker), then spend the rest of the pool on Roku's {R}{R}{R}: +3/+0 on the double-striking Zuko. Plan this for commander damage and a one-turn kill, and leave nothing in the pool, because it empties at end of combat. _Evidence: Game 0023, round 5 (turn 19): Zuko and Party Thrasher attacked, making 10 R (6 from Roku, 4 from firebending). Embercleave cost {2}{R}{R} out of the pool, and two Roku pumps made Zuko an 11/7 double striker that knocked out Obeka (13 life). In round 7 (turn 24), four Roku pumps took Zuko to 19 power and he killed Rin and Seri from 17._
