# Lessons from 2026-09-21_123655_pod-the-wise-mothman-edgar-markov-tidus-_0031, 2026-09-21_123657_pod-miirym-fire-lord-zuko-yuriko-fire-lo_0032, 2026-09-21_124814_pod-fire-lord-zuko-terra-hearthhull-zaxa_0033, 2026-09-21_132410_pod-mr-house-fire-lord-zuko-choco-feloth_0034

- **sequencing** Foretell Sozin's Comet as soon as you have a spare {2}. Once Zuko and Isshin are both on the battlefield, cast the Comet from exile for {2}{R} before combat, then attack with everything. Isshin doubles every firebending trigger. Pour that pool into Electrodominance with a big X at an opponent you can target, and use its free cast to put a hard-to-cast card like Ozai into play from your hand. _Evidence: Game 4. In round 5 (turn 19) the pilot cast Isshin and foretold the Comet off Sol Ring. In round 6 (turn 23) it cast the Comet from exile, which triggered Zuko's counters, and attacked with Zuko, Goliath Daydreamer and Isshin. The doubled firebending made 26 red. Electrodominance with X=22 took Choco from 22 to 0 and cast Ozai for free. Ozai had been stuck in hand all game because the pilot had only one black source._
- **how_a_card_works** Ozai turns your unspent mana red instead of letting it empty, and he has flying and indestructible while you hold 6 or more unspent mana. When an opponent aims destroy removal at him, tap lands in response until your pool reaches 6. Keep at least 6 floating between turns. _Evidence: Game 3, round 7 (turn 25). Hearthhull cast Putrefy on Ozai while the pilot held 5 mana. The pilot tapped a Mountain in response to reach 6, so Ozai was indestructible and Putrefy did nothing. The auditor then confirmed the firebending mana stays in the pool at end of combat while Ozai is out, which left the pilot with 14 {R}._
- **commander_timing** Cast Norin the Wary before Zuko, or together with him. Norin exiles himself whenever any player casts a spell or attacks, and he comes back at every end step. That puts a +1/+1 counter on each of your creatures on every player's turn at no cost, so Zuko's firebending grows before he ever attacks. _Evidence: Game 2. Norin came down in round 2 (turn 7) and Zuko as a 2/4 in round 3 (turn 11). Norin returned at the end steps of turns 11, 12 and 13, so by round 4 (turn 15) Zuko had 3 counters and firebending 5. The auditor confirmed he was a 6/8 with Embercleave._
- **combat** On an attack turn, spend the firebending mana in combat. Embercleave costs {1} less for each attacker, so flash it in on Zuko. Keep a {R} from the pool for Redirect Lightning (paying 5 life as its additional cost) so a bounce or removal spell can't stop the swing. _Evidence: Game 2, round 4 (turn 15). Zuko and Laelia attacked, and firebending made 5 {R}. Embercleave cost {2}{R}{R}, paid with 4 of it. Miirym answered with Cyclonic Rift on Zuko, and the leftover {R} paid for Redirect Lightning, which moved the Rift onto Yuriko. The auditor upheld that Zuko connected for 12 commander damage._
- **sequencing** Foretell Delayed Blast Fireball early. Party Thrasher gives spells cast from exile convoke, so your creatures can pay part of the {4}{R}{R} foretell cost. Cast from exile, the Fireball deals 5 to each opponent and each of their creatures, which makes it a one-sided wipe. After it resolves, opponents have lost life, so Light Up the Stage costs only its {R} spectacle cost that turn. _Evidence: Game 1. In round 4 (turn 14) the pilot cast Thrasher and foretold the Fireball. In round 5 (turn 18) it cast the Fireball from exile, tapping Thrasher for convoke. It killed nine creatures across three opponents and took 5 life from each. The pilot then cast Light Up the Stage (chosen from exile with Thrasher) for {R} from Shinka._
- **threat_assessment** When you have free fights, send them at the opponent whose board you've already flagged as able to kill you, not at the player with the most life. Crystal Barricade prevents noncombat damage only to its controller's other creatures, so a fight can still kill the Barricade itself. That also removes its player hexproof. _Evidence: Game 4, round 6 (turn 23). The pilot's notes flagged Felothar's Tree of Perdition, which could swap the pilot's 37 life for its 13 toughness. Yet all three Agni Kai casts went at Mr. House, at 36 life, and his Iron Mastiff. On the next turn Felothar used the Tree to drop the pilot to 13, then killed him with Welcoming Vampire and the unblockable Crystal Barricade._
