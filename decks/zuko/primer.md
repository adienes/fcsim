# Fire Lord Zuko

## Gameplan
Mardu in name, mostly red in practice. Zuko attacks and adds red equal to his power. Each spell you cast from exile, and each permanent you control that enters from exile, puts a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control. Impulse draw, foretell and airbend turn most plays into team-wide counters. Stack counters, then turn combat mana into lethal.

## Opening hands
- Mulligan a two-land seven unless it has a rock (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, a Talisman) and two plays castable off three sources. Even then, play the rock on turn 2 and expect to sit on three sources: the payoffs cost 4 to 6. Legion Leadership and Valakut Awakening double as lands.
- Check that the hand makes {R}{W}{B} by turn 3 or 4. White runs short, and Zuko, Isshin and Swords to Plowshares need it. With no white source and only one or two mono-red spells, mulligan. The first mulligan is free.
- Early exile engines make a hand strong: Norin the Wary, Dream Devourer, Prosper, Tome-Bound, Party Thrasher.

## Early turns and the commander
- Cast haste creatures in main phase 1: Laelia, the Blade Reforged, Iroh, Dragon of the West, Headliner Scarlett, Hellkite Charger.
- Dream Devourer gives your hand foretell, and foretold cards are later cast from exile.
- Seen once: cast Zuko before playing anything exiled by Party Thrasher, Bonehoard Dracosaur or Light Up the Stage. Lands played from exile count. Hold exiled flash cards such as Embercleave for combat.
- Protect Zuko before overextending: Mithril Coat, Flawless Maneuver (free while you control a commander), Ultimate Magic: Holy.
- If Zuko dies, send him to the command zone and budget {R}{W}{B} plus tax first (seen once).

## How it wins
Firebending mana empties at end of combat. Before every Zuko attack, know how you will spend it: Embercleave (flash, {1} less per attacker), Electrodominance with a big X (it also casts a free spell), Invoke Calamity, or The Last Agni Kai.
- Electro, Assaulting Battery and Leyline Tyrant keep red past combat, and Ozai, the Phoenix King turns lost mana red. With any of them out, combat mana pays for Aggravated Assault.
- Seen once: with Avatar Roku, Firebender out, put Zuko's trigger on the stack first so Roku's six red resolves first, then spend it on Roku's +3/+0 on Zuko.
- Seen once: Chandra's Ignition also hits Zuko, so fire it only for lethal.
- With Zuko out, always take Goliath Daydreamer's free recast.
- Extra combats: Aggravated Assault, Hellkite Charger, Great Train Heist. Burst: Embercleave, Two-Handed Axe, Bulk Up, Legion Leadership.
- Avatar's Wrath is one-sided. Target Zuko to keep him, and each {2} recast triggers Zuko and Appa, Steadfast Guardian. Damning Verdict spares creatures with counters.

## What to answer, what to ignore
Removal is thin: Swords to Plowshares, Fires of Mount Doom, Untimely Malfunction, Redirect Lightning, The Last Agni Kai, and Delayed Blast Fireball (5 damage from exile). Save it for what stops Zuko's attacks: big blockers, fogs, and removal aimed at him.
- Redirect Lightning and Untimely Malfunction turn single-target removal away. Malfunction also stops blockers on the lethal turn.
- Bojuka Bog answers graveyard decks.
- Race slow value engines instead of answering them.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- Keeping two-landers with no rock and stalling on three mana with 5-drops in hand.
- Attacking with Zuko with no plan for the firebending mana.
- Casting haste creatures after combat.
- Letting Ozai die (seen once): he is indestructible with six or more unspent mana, so tap lands in response to removal.
