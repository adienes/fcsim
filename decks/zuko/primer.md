# Fire Lord Zuko

## Gameplan
Mardu in name, mono-red in practice. Zuko attacks and makes red equal to his power. Whenever you cast a spell from exile or a permanent enters from exile, he puts a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control. Impulse draw, foretell and airbend turn most plays into a team-wide counter. Stack counters, then turn combat mana into lethal.

## Opening hands
- Mulligan a two-land seven unless it has a rock (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, a Talisman) and at least two plays castable off those three sources. Play the rock on turn 2 and expect to sit on three sources for a while, because the payoffs cost 4 to 6. Legion Leadership and Valakut Awakening double as lands.
- Early exile engines make a hand strong: Norin the Wary, Dream Devourer, Prosper, Tome-Bound, Party Thrasher.

## Early turns and the commander
- Cast haste creatures in main phase 1: Laelia, the Blade Reforged, Iroh, Dragon of the West, Headliner Scarlett, Hellkite Charger. Cast after combat, they lose their first attack and its triggers.
- Cast Norin the Wary before Zuko (seen once). Any spell cast or any creature attacking exiles Norin, and each end-step return triggers Zuko.
- Dream Devourer gives your hand foretell, so foretold cards are later cast from exile.
- Party Thrasher (seen once): have Zuko out first, because a land played from exile triggers him. Its exiled noncreature spells have convoke.
- Protect Zuko before overextending: Mithril Coat (flash), Flawless Maneuver (free while you control a commander), Ultimate Magic: Holy.

## How it wins
Firebending mana empties at end of combat. Before every Zuko attack, know how you will spend it:
- Embercleave, flashed in for {1} less per attacker.
- Electrodominance for a big X, which also casts a free spell.
- Invoke Calamity or The Last Agni Kai.

More ways to use combat mana and win:
- Electro, Assaulting Battery and Leyline Tyrant keep red past combat. Ozai, the Phoenix King turns lost mana red. With any of them out, combat mana pays for Aggravated Assault.
- Avatar Roku, Firebender (seen once): resolve Roku's six red first. Spend it on {R}{R}{R} pumps on Zuko while his firebending trigger waits, because X is counted on resolution.
- Sozin's Comet (seen once): with Isshin, Two Heavens as One out, cast it from foretell. Firebending then triggers twice, so hold an X spell.
- Storm King's Thunder (seen once): pay for it with firebending red. Then cast Chandra's Ignition from lands in main 2, with no instant or sorcery in between. Ignition hits your creatures too, so fire it only for lethal.
- While Zuko is out, always take Goliath Daydreamer's free recast, even at X=0 (seen once).
- Extra combats: Aggravated Assault, Hellkite Charger, Great Train Heist.
- Burst: Embercleave, Two-Handed Axe, Bulk Up, Legion Leadership.
- Avatar's Wrath is one-sided. Target Zuko to keep him, and each {2} recast triggers Zuko and Appa, Steadfast Guardian.
- Damning Verdict spares creatures with counters.

## What to answer, what to ignore
Removal is thin: Swords to Plowshares, Fires of Mount Doom, Untimely Malfunction, Redirect Lightning, The Last Agni Kai, and Delayed Blast Fireball (5 damage when cast from exile). Save it for what stops Zuko's attacks: big blockers, fogs, and removal aimed at him.
- Redirect Lightning and Untimely Malfunction turn single-target removal away. Malfunction also stops blockers on the lethal turn.
- Bojuka Bog answers graveyard decks.
- Race slow value engines instead of answering them.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- Keeping two-landers with no rock and stalling on three mana with 5-drops in hand.
- Attacking with Zuko with no plan for the firebending mana.
- Casting haste creatures after combat.
- Letting Ozai die to removal (seen once). He is indestructible with six or more unspent mana, so tap lands in response. On your own turn, don't spend your pool below six.
