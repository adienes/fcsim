# Fire Lord Zuko

## Gameplan
Mardu in name, mostly red in practice. Zuko attacks and adds red equal to his power. Every spell you cast from exile, and every permanent you control that enters from exile, puts a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control. Impulse draw, foretell and airbend turn most plays into team-wide counters. Stack counters, then convert combat mana into lethal.

## Opening hands
- Mulligan a two-land seven unless it has a rock (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, a Talisman) and two plays castable off those three sources. Even then, play the rock on turn 2 and expect to sit on three sources for several turns: the payoffs cost 4 to 6. Legion Leadership and Valakut Awakening double as lands.
- Check that the hand makes {R}{W}{B} by turn 3 or 4. White is the short colour, and Zuko, Isshin and Swords to Plowshares all need it. A hand whose lands are only Mountains, Swamps and red duals can cast only its mono-red spells; if that is one or two spells, mulligan. The first mulligan is free.
- Early exile engines make a hand strong: Norin the Wary, Dream Devourer, Prosper, Tome-Bound, Party Thrasher.

## Early turns and the commander
- Cast haste creatures in main phase 1: Laelia, the Blade Reforged, Iroh, Dragon of the West, Headliner Scarlett, Hellkite Charger. After combat they lose an attack, and Laelia loses her exile trigger.
- Cast Zuko before playing anything already exiled by Party Thrasher, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Light Up the Stage or Commune with Lava. Lands played from exile count. Hold exiled flash cards like Embercleave for combat.
- Party Thrasher is a full engine: each first main phase discard a dead card, exile two, take a land or cheap spell. Noncreature spells cast from exile have convoke, so tap creatures to pay.
- Dream Devourer gives your whole hand foretell, and foretold cards are later cast from exile.
- Cast Norin the Wary first. He re-enters from exile at every end step he leaves, giving the team a counter each time with Zuko out.
- Protect Zuko: Mithril Coat, Flawless Maneuver, Ultimate Magic: Holy. Redirect Lightning changes the target of any single-target spell *or ability* — Saga chapters and ETB removal included. {R} plus 5 life is a fine price.
- If Zuko dies or is exiled, move him to the command zone; he is never lost. But do not burn a turn recasting a 2/4 with no haste onto an empty board. Spend that turn on bodies and on exiled cards whose window is closing, and recast him on a turn with room for both.

## How it wins
Firebending mana empties at end of combat. Before every Zuko attack, know the sink: Embercleave (flash, {1} less per attacker), Electrodominance with a big X (it also casts a free spell from hand), Invoke Calamity, The Last Agni Kai. Hold X spells for combat.
- Electrostatic Field effects aside, Assaulting Battery, Leyline Tyrant and Ozai keep or convert that red past combat. With any of them out, combat mana buys Aggravated Assault.
- Isshin doubles every firebending trigger; a Sozin's Comet foretold cheap and cast from exile behind him is the biggest turn available (seen once).
- Storm King's Thunder then Chandra's Ignition on your biggest creature hits each opponent X+1 times. Cast nothing else between them. Ignition hits Zuko too — fire it only for lethal.
- With Zuko out, always take Goliath Daydreamer's free recast, even at X=0.
- Extra combats: Aggravated Assault, Hellkite Charger, Great Train Heist. Burst: Embercleave, Two-Handed Axe, Bulk Up, Legion Leadership.
- Avatar's Wrath is not a wrath — everything returns for {2}, and only your casts trigger Zuko. Airbend your own cheap permanents on purpose. Damning Verdict spares creatures with counters.
- On a turn an opponent will take 7+, leave Spinerock Knoll untapped; its {R} can come from firebending mana.

## What to answer, what to ignore
Removal is thin: Swords to Plowshares, Fires of Mount Doom, Untimely Malfunction, Redirect Lightning, The Last Agni Kai, Delayed Blast Fireball (5 from exile). Save it for what stops attacks: big blockers, fogs, removal on Zuko. Untimely Malfunction also clears blockers on the lethal turn. Bojuka Bog answers graveyards. Race slow value engines; do not answer them.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- Keeping two-landers with no rock, stalling on three mana with 5-drops in hand.
- Attacking with Zuko with no plan for the firebending mana.
- Casting haste creatures after combat.
- Exiling more than the turn's mana can pay for. Every impulse exile has a deadline — count before choosing X on Commune with Lava or March of Reckless Joy, and never empty your hand to Hex Magic on a turn you may not survive.
- Letting Ozai die: he is indestructible with six or more unspent mana, so tap lands in response to removal (seen once).
