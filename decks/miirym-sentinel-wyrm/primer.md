# Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm

## Gameplan
Ramp early, cut the cost of Dragons, then land Miirym and let every nontoken Dragon come with a nonlegendary copy. Each copy doubles that Dragon's enters-trigger and is another flier. Tokens do not trigger Miirym, so only cast Dragons get copied. The deck wins in the air, or with damage dealt as creatures enter.

## Opening hands
Keep hands with 3–4 lands and a green ramp piece: Rampant Growth, Farseek, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or Scaled Nurturer. Green mana on turns 1–2 matters most. Cost reducers are also strong keeps: Dragonspeaker Shaman, Dragonlord's Servant, Sarkhan, Soul Aflame and Urza's Incubator (name Dragon). A hand of only six- and seven-drop Dragons is a mulligan. Korlessa, Scale Singer on turn 2 lets you cast Dragons off the top of your library.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp first, then play card-draw engines before your threats: Kindred Discovery (name Dragon), Elemental Bond, Garruk's Uprising, Temur Ascendancy and Dragon's Hoard. Miirym comes down around turn 4–5. She has ward {2}. Protect her with Swiftfoot Boots, or cast her with Jade Orb of Dragonkind mana for hexproof. Hold Heroic Intervention once she and your board matter.

Once Miirym is out, follow with Atarka, World Render if you can. Atarka gives every attacking Dragon double strike, so Miirym's 6/6 flying body deals 12 commander damage in one swing. Aim it at the player with no fliers or reach (seen once). Rhythm of the Wild, Temur Ascendancy, Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind and Dragon Tempest give haste, so your new Dragons swing the turn they land.

## How it wins
- **Enters-damage.** Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas and Dragon Tempest punish every Dragon that enters, and Miirym's copies double the triggers. Twinflame Tyrant doubles all damage dealt to opponents. Lozhan, Dragons' Legacy and Drakuseth, Maw of Flames add direct damage.
- **Air force.** Lathliss, Dragon Queen and Utvara Hellkite make 5/5 and 6/6 Dragon tokens. Thrakkus the Butcher doubles Dragon power. Return of the Wildspeaker gives +3/+3, or refills your hand.
- **Mana bursts.** Klauth, Unrivaled Ancient, Savage Ventmaw, Goldspan Dragon and Old Gnawbone produce mana when attacking or dealing combat damage. Spend it on more Dragons after combat. Dracogenesis makes every Dragon free.
- **Finding pieces.** Sarkhan's Triumph and Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon tutor for a Dragon. Ureni of the Unwritten and Herald's Horn dig. Haven of the Spirit Dragon and Earthquake Dragon recur threats.

## What to answer, what to ignore
Your answers are few: Beast Within, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Counterspell and Negate. Save them for board wipes, for a combo, and for a board that can kill you. Ignore small value engines, because your fliers outrace them.

Overload Cyclonic Rift ({6}{U}) in your own main phase before combat, after the go-wide opponent has attacked and tapped out. It bounces every blocker and erases their tokens, so your fliers swing into an empty board (seen once).

Blasphemous Act kills your own Dragons too. Cast it only when you are behind on board, or with Heroic Intervention held up to save your creatures. Rhythm of the Wild makes your creature spells uncounterable, so land it before a big turn against blue.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Tapping out for another Dragon while an opponent's board can kill you in one attack.** A new 8-drop blocks one creature, not 70 power. On your turn, spend Beast Within or Chaos Warp on their key piece. Otherwise hold up 3 mana for Beast Within and {U}{U} for Counterspell (seen once).
- **Assuming a Rift on an attacker is safe.** Target-changing spells such as Redirect Lightning or Deflecting Swat move your spell to a new target. The original attacker then stays in combat. Plan your blocks and life total for that before relying on a single-target Rift (seen once).
