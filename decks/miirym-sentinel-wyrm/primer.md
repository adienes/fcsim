# Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm

## Gameplan
Ramp early and cut the cost of Dragons. Then land Miirym. Every nontoken Dragon that enters, whether cast or put onto the battlefield by Ureni of the Unwritten or Sarkhan Unbroken, brings a nonlegendary token copy. The copy doubles that Dragon's enters-trigger and adds another flier. Tokens do not trigger Miirym. You win in the air, or with damage dealt as creatures enter.

## Opening hands
Keep 3–4 lands plus ramp: Rampant Growth, Farseek, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or Scaled Nurturer. Green mana on turns 1–2 matters most. Cost reducers are strong keeps: Dragonspeaker Shaman, Dragonlord's Servant, Sarkhan, Soul Aflame, and Urza's Incubator naming Dragon. Mulligan a hand of only six- and seven-drops. Korlessa, Scale Singer on turn 2 lets you cast Dragons off the top of your library.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp first. Play draw engines before threats: Kindred Discovery (name Dragon), Elemental Bond, Garruk's Uprising, Temur Ascendancy and Dragon's Hoard. Miirym lands around turn 4–5 with ward {2}. Protect her with Swiftfoot Boots, or cast her using Jade Orb of Dragonkind mana for hexproof. Hold Heroic Intervention once your board matters. Panharmonicon, or Roaming Throne naming Dragon, makes Miirym trigger twice, so you get two copies.

After Miirym, follow up with Atarka, World Render. Every attacking Dragon gains double strike, so Miirym alone deals 12 commander damage in one swing. Point it at the player with no fliers or reach (seen once). Rhythm of the Wild, Temur Ascendancy, Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind and Dragon Tempest give haste, so new Dragons attack the turn they land.

## How it wins
- **Enters-damage.** Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas and Dragon Tempest punish every entering Dragon, and Miirym's copies double the triggers. Twinflame Tyrant doubles damage dealt to opponents. Lozhan, Dragons' Legacy and Drakuseth, Maw of Flames add direct damage.
- **Air force.** Lathliss, Dragon Queen and Utvara Hellkite make 5/5 and 6/6 Dragon tokens. A Miirym copy of Lathliss doubles her output. Thrakkus the Butcher doubles Dragon power. Return of the Wildspeaker gives +3/+3, or refills your hand.
- **Mana bursts.** Klauth, Unrivaled Ancient, Savage Ventmaw, Goldspan Dragon and Old Gnawbone make mana in combat. Spend it on Dragons after combat. Dracogenesis makes Dragons free.
- **Finding pieces.** Sarkhan's Triumph and Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon tutor. Ureni and Herald's Horn dig. Haven of the Spirit Dragon and Earthquake Dragon recur threats.

## What to answer, what to ignore
Your answers are few: Beast Within, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Counterspell and Negate. Save them for wipes, combos, and boards that can kill you. Ignore small value engines, because your fliers outrace them.

Overload Cyclonic Rift ({6}{U}) in your own main phase before combat, after the go-wide opponent has attacked and tapped out. It bounces every blocker and erases their tokens, so your fliers swing into an empty board (seen once).

Blasphemous Act kills your Dragons too. Cast it when behind, or with Heroic Intervention up; indestructible creatures survive the 13 damage. With Wrathful Red Dragon on the battlefield, each of your Dragons hit by Blasphemous Act deals 13 damage to any non-Dragon target. Land Rhythm of the Wild before a big turn against blue.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Tapping out for another big Dragon when one opponent's board can kill you in a single attack.** A new 8-drop such as Ancient Silver Dragon blocks one creature, not 70 power. On your turn, spend Beast Within or Chaos Warp on their key piece. Otherwise keep 3 mana open for Beast Within and {U}{U} for Counterspell.
- **Assuming a single-target Rift on an attacker is safe.** Redirect Lightning or Deflecting Swat moves your spell to a new target, and the original attacker stays in combat. Plan your blocks and life total for that (seen once).
