# Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm

## Gameplan
Ramp, cut Dragon costs, land Miirym, then deploy. Every nontoken Dragon that enters brings a nonlegendary token copy — whether you cast it or put it onto the battlefield with Ureni of the Unwritten or Sarkhan Unbroken's −8. The copy repeats the enters-trigger and adds a flier. Tokens do not trigger Miirym. You win in the air, or on damage dealt as Dragons enter.

## Opening hands
Keep 3–4 lands plus ramp: Rampant Growth, Farseek, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Encroaching Dragonstorm; Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Scaled Nurturer. Green on turns 1–2 matters most. Cost reducers are strong keeps: Dragonspeaker Shaman, Dragonlord's Servant, Sarkhan Soul Aflame, Urza's Incubator naming Dragon. Korlessa, Scale Singer on turn 2 casts Dragons off the top of your library. Mulligan a hand of only six- and seven-drops.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp, then draw engines before threats: Kindred Discovery (name Dragon), Elemental Bond, Garruk's Uprising, Temur Ascendancy, Dragon's Hoard.

Miirym is the multiplier, not another body: a Dragon that enters while she is absent is one card. After a wrath, recast her the first turn you can pay the tax, before Ureni or another expensive Dragon. Get Jade Orb of Dragonkind down early and spend its {G} only on Dragon creature spells — that is what buys the counter and hexproof until your next turn; pay green instants from a Forest instead. Swiftfoot Boots and Heroic Intervention are the backup. Panharmonicon, or Roaming Throne naming Dragon, doubles Miirym's trigger for two copies.

Temur Ascendancy and Dragon Tempest haste the copies too. Rhythm of the Wild's riot and Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind haste only the card you cast.

With Sarkhan, Soul Aflame, decline his copy trigger until you have cast the Dragons that want his {1} discount — he loses it while he is a copy. On the turn Miirym enters, copy Miirym herself: for the rest of that turn each nontoken Dragon makes two token copies (seen once).

## How it wins
- **Enters-damage.** Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas and Dragon Tempest punish every Dragon that enters, and copies double it. Scourge triggers on itself: with Miirym, one Scourge gives three triggers — its own, the token's, and its own again when the token enters — each sized as it resolves. Put its trigger on the stack before you call an attack lethal; triggers still resolve off last known information if Scourge is removed. Twinflame Tyrant doubles damage to opponents; Lozhan and Drakuseth add reach.
- **Air force.** Lathliss and Utvara Hellkite build 5/5s and 6/6s; a Lathliss copy doubles her output. Thrakkus doubles Dragon power; Return of the Wildspeaker pumps or refills.
- **Combat mana.** Klauth, Savage Ventmaw, Goldspan Dragon, Old Gnawbone. Spend it on Dragons after blocks. Dracogenesis makes them free.
- **Finding pieces.** Sarkhan's Triumph and Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon tutor; Ureni and Herald's Horn dig; Haven of the Spirit Dragon rebuys.
- **Treasures.** Hold them through declare blockers, then dump them into Lathliss's {1}{R}: it pumps every attacking Dragon, so blocked ones kill blockers and unblocked ones carry the damage. Pumping before blocks only tells the defender how to block.
- Once Miirym is down, Atarka, World Render is the follow-up: copied Atarka gives attacking Dragons double strike, so Miirym alone is 12 commander damage. Point it at the player with no fliers or reach (seen once).

## What to answer, what to ignore
Your answers are few: Beast Within, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Counterspell, Negate. Save them for wipes, combos, and boards that can kill you. Ignore small value engines; your fliers outrace them.

Overload Cyclonic Rift ({6}{U}) in your own main phase, after the go-wide opponent has attacked and tapped out, before your combat. It clears blockers and erases tokens.

Blasphemous Act kills your Dragons too — cast it from behind, or with Heroic Intervention up. With Wrathful Red Dragon out, each Dragon it hits throws 13 at a non-Dragon target. Land Rhythm of the Wild before a big turn against blue.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Tapping out for another big Dragon when an opponent can kill you in one attack.** An 8-drop blocks one creature; it does not stop 70 power. Either spend Beast Within or Chaos Warp on their key piece on your turn, or hold 3 mana for Beast Within and {U}{U} for Counterspell.
- **Trusting a single-target Rift on an attacker.** Redirected spells resolve against the new target only; the attacker stays in combat. Plan blocks and life accordingly (seen once).
