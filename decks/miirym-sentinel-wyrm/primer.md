# Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm

## Gameplan
Ramp early, cut the cost of Dragons, then land Miirym. Every nontoken Dragon that enters brings a nonlegendary token copy of itself. This works whether you cast it or put it onto the battlefield with Ureni of the Unwritten or Sarkhan Unbroken's −8. The copy doubles that Dragon's enters-trigger and adds another flier. Tokens do not trigger Miirym. You win in the air, or with damage dealt as creatures enter.

## Opening hands
Keep 3–4 lands plus ramp:
- **Spells:** Rampant Growth, Farseek, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Encroaching Dragonstorm.
- **Rocks and dorks:** Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Scaled Nurturer.

Green mana on turns 1–2 matters most. Cost reducers are strong keeps: Dragonspeaker Shaman, Dragonlord's Servant, Sarkhan, Soul Aflame, and Urza's Incubator naming Dragon. Korlessa, Scale Singer on turn 2 lets you cast Dragons off the top of your library. Mulligan a hand of only six- and seven-drops.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp first, then draw engines before threats: Kindred Discovery (name Dragon), Elemental Bond, Garruk's Uprising, Temur Ascendancy, Dragon's Hoard.

Miirym lands around turn 4–5 with ward {2}. Protect her with Swiftfoot Boots, or cast her with Jade Orb of Dragonkind mana for hexproof until your next turn. Hold Heroic Intervention once your board matters. Panharmonicon, or Roaming Throne naming Dragon, doubles Miirym's trigger, so you get two copies.

Temur Ascendancy and Dragon Tempest give haste to the copies too. Rhythm of the Wild's riot and Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind haste only the card you cast.

Atarka, World Render gives attacking Dragons double strike, so Miirym alone deals 12 commander damage in one swing.

## How it wins
- **Enters-damage.** Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas and Dragon Tempest punish every entering Dragon, and copies double the triggers. Twinflame Tyrant doubles damage dealt to opponents. Lozhan, Dragons' Legacy and Drakuseth, Maw of Flames add direct damage.
- **Air force.** Lathliss, Dragon Queen and Utvara Hellkite make 5/5 and 6/6 Dragon tokens; a Lathliss copy doubles her output. Thrakkus the Butcher doubles Dragon power. Return of the Wildspeaker pumps or refills.
- **Mana bursts.** Klauth, Unrivaled Ancient, Savage Ventmaw, Goldspan Dragon and Old Gnawbone make combat mana; spend it on Dragons afterward. Dracogenesis makes Dragons free.
- **Finding pieces.** Sarkhan's Triumph and Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon tutor. Ureni and Herald's Horn dig. Haven of the Spirit Dragon returns a Dragon to hand, and Earthquake Dragon returns itself.

## What to answer, what to ignore
Your answers are few: Beast Within, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Counterspell, Negate. Save them for wipes, combos and boards that can kill you. Ignore small value engines, because your fliers outrace them.

Overload Cyclonic Rift ({6}{U}) in your main phase before combat, after the go-wide opponent has attacked and tapped out. It bounces every blocker and erases their tokens.

Blasphemous Act kills your Dragons too. Cast it when behind, or with Heroic Intervention up. With Wrathful Red Dragon out, each of your Dragons it hits deals 13 damage to any non-Dragon target. Land Rhythm of the Wild before a big turn against blue.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Tapping out for another big Dragon when one opponent's board can kill you in a single attack.** A new 8-drop such as Ancient Silver Dragon blocks one creature. It does not stop a swing of 70 power. On your turn, spend Beast Within or Chaos Warp on their key piece. Otherwise keep 3 mana open for Beast Within and {U}{U} for Counterspell.
- **Trusting a single-target Rift on an attacker.** Redirect Lightning or Deflecting Swat can move it, and the attacker stays in combat (seen once).
