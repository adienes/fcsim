- **how_a_card_works** Count Scourge of Valkas's own enters trigger. With Miirym out, one Scourge gives three damage triggers — the card's own, the token copy's own, and the card's again when the token enters — each equal to the number of Dragons you control as it resolves. Put the card's own trigger on the stack yourself before you declare an attack lethal, and remember the triggers still resolve off last known information if the Scourge is removed in response. _Evidence: Game 0062 turn 26: the real Scourge's own enters trigger was never put on the stack (only Garruk's Uprising, Miirym's copy trigger and Sarkhan's).
