# Lessons from 2026-09-21_182210_pod-miirym-obeka-wilhelt-fire-lord-zuko_0042

- **threat_assessment** On your own turn, add up the flying power and attack triggers that could come at you next turn. If that total is at or near your life, spend Chaos Warp or Beast Within on the biggest attacker (for example Drakuseth, Maw of Flames, which deals 7 in combat plus 4+3+3 from its attack trigger), not on a value engine. Before choosing a target, check whose library the engine actually hits. _Evidence: Game 0042, round 8: at 18 life, the pilot Chaos Warped Laughing Jasper Flint 'which was stealing cards off the top of my library'. Flint's triggers targeted Wilhelt's library, not Miirym's. Next turn, Obeka's Drakuseth killed the Dragon token and dealt 3 to Miirym with its trigger. Then Drakuseth (7), Skyline Despot (5) and Savage Ventmaw (4) left her at -2. Warping Drakuseth would have removed 10 of that damage and saved the token._
