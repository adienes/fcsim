# Lessons from 2026-09-20_184504_pod-fire-lord-zuko-lathril-miirym-wilhel_0006, 2026-09-20_222947_pod-fire-lord-zuko-lathril-miirym-wilhel_0007, 2026-09-21_123657_pod-miirym-fire-lord-zuko-yuriko-fire-lo_0032

- **threat_assessment** Once one opponent's board is big enough to kill you in a single attack, stop tapping out for another big Dragon. On your turn, either spend Beast Within or Chaos Warp on their key piece, or keep 3 mana open for Beast Within and 2 for Counterspell. A new 8-drop blocks one creature. It does not stop a swing of 70 power. _Evidence: Game 0006, round 8: Lathril had just killed Zuko with a 38-power swing and had about 25 Elves plus Crown of Skemfar. Miirym tapped out for Ancient Silver Dragon, leaving only Haven of the Spirit Dragon untapped, with Beast Within and Counterspell in hand. In round 9 Lathril swung 72 power at it and it died from 28 life, unable to pay {2}{G} for Beast Within on Crown of Skemfar. The pilot's own note: 'never tap out with Lathril's board untouched.'_
- **interaction** Overload Cyclonic Rift ({6}{U}) in your own main phase, after the go-wide opponent has attacked and tapped out, and before your combat. That bounces every blocker, erases their tokens, and lets your fliers swing into an empty board. _Evidence: Game 0007, round 9: Miirym overloaded Rift while all of Lathril's Elves were tapped, which wiped every token. Utvara Hellkite and a token then dealt Zuko exactly lethal, and three attack triggers left Miirym with seven creatures. Lathril died the next turn to a 36-damage swing._
- **commander_timing** Once Miirym is on the battlefield, follow up with Atarka, World Render. Miirym copies Atarka, and each Atarka gives every attacking Dragon double strike, so the 6/6 flier deals 12 commander damage in one swing. Point that damage at the player with no fliers or reach. _Evidence: Game 0006, round 6: Atarka was copied by Miirym, Elemental Bond drew two cards, and Miirym hit Lathril (who had no flying or reach) for 6 plus 6, leaving 28 life and 12 commander damage._
- **how_a_card_works** If an opponent changes the target of your spell (Redirect Lightning, Deflecting Swat), the spell resolves against the new target only. When Cyclonic Rift gets redirected, the original attacker stays in combat, so plan your blocks and life total for that. Fire Lord Zuko decks in these pods carry target-changing spells, so expect them before you spend Rift on an attacker. _Evidence: Game 0032, turn 15: Miirym Rifted Fire Lord Zuko, who was attacking with Embercleave. Zuko's Redirect Lightning moved the Rift onto Yuriko. Miirym bounced Zuko anyway, and the auditor reversed it. Zuko's 12 double-strike damage then counted, putting Miirym at 21 life with 12 commander damage._
