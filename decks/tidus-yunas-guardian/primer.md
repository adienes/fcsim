# Tidus, Yuna's Guardian

## Gameplan
Put +1/+1 counters on a few creatures, make them hard to block, and turn combat damage into cards and proliferate. Doublers: Hardened Scales, Kami of Whispered Hopes, The Earth Crystal, Innkeeper's Talent at level 3. Cards come from Tidus's Cheer, Bred for the Hunt, Kutzil, Fathom Mage, Chasm Skulker and O'aka. Blitzball Stadium's Go for the Goal draws a card per kind of counter on its target, so add kinds first with Arwen or Protection Magic.

## Opening hands
- Keep three or more lands that make two colors, plus ramp or a cheap counter piece.
- Ramp: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Farseek, Three Visits, Gyre Sage, Incubation Druid.
- Farseek finds Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista, Prairie Stream or Radiant Grove.
- Three Visits finds Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista or Radiant Grove.
- Many lands enter tapped, so plan the curve around them.
- Mulligan hands of only lands and six-drops.

## Early turns and the commander
- Tidus moves counters only between your own creatures, and Cheer needs a creature with counters to connect. Cast a counter source first: Shelinda, Scholar of New Horizons, Duskshell Crawler, Tromell or Innkeeper's Talent. Forge of Heroes gives Tidus a counter the turn he enters.
- Thrummingbird and Grateful Apparition proliferate early. Incubation Druid with a +1/+1 counter taps for three.
- Tidus, Maester Seymour, Kimahri, Fight Rigging, Innkeeper's Talent and The Ozolith trigger at beginning of combat. Resolve Tidus's move last.

## How it wins
- **Evasion:** Herald of Secret Streams makes every creature with a +1/+1 counter unblockable. Rikku makes a creature unblockable whenever it gets counters. Duskshell Crawler and Sphere Grid give trample.
- **Multipliers:** Chocobo Knights gives double strike to creatures with counters. Wakka spreads counters at end step. Tekuthal doubles proliferate. Inexorable Tide and Dreamtide Whale proliferate off spells.
- **Late:** Sin, Unending Cataclysm gathers your counters into one flying trampler. Walking Ballista finishes low life totals. Fight Rigging casts its hidden card free once you control a creature with 7 or more power.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- **Board wipes are the real threat.** Hold Counterspell or An Offer You Can't Refuse. Ripples of Potential phases out what it proliferates, dodging exile. Inspiring Call and Protection Magic stop "destroy" and damage, not exile.
- **Your sweepers:** Damning Verdict is one-sided when all your creatures have counters. Farewell and Promise of Loyalty reset a losing board.
- **Spot removal:** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Destroy Evil, Collective Effort, Summon: Ixion, Summon: Yojimbo.
- **Ignore** ground blockers once Herald is out. Cast Kutzil before a big turn: opponents can't cast spells during your turn.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Scattering counters.** Before combat, pile every source onto one evasive or trampling creature: The Ozolith, Fight Rigging, Innkeeper's Talent, Blitzball Stadium's support, Elspeth's +1, and Tidus's move. Let Sagas and creatures about to die hand their counters to The Ozolith. Then swing for the kill or for 21 commander damage.
- **Using removal on your own turn.** Keep one white open on opponents' turns for Swords to Plowshares or Path to Exile. Exile an attacking commander after a pump like double strike is announced: their trick fizzles and the commander leaves combat.
- **Forgetting Tidus moves any counter (seen once).** Shift a stun counter onto a creature that isn't attacking.
- **Advancing Sagas by accident (seen once).** Proliferate adds lore counters. Leave Sagas out unless you want the next chapter.
- **Missing the O'aka loop (seen once).** After chapter I of Summon: Valefor, Summon: Ixion or Summon: Yojimbo resolves, tap O'aka to remove its lore counter and draw. Chapter I triggers again next precombat main phase.
