# Tidus, Yuna's Guardian

## Gameplan
Stack +1/+1 counters on a few creatures, give them evasion or trample, and convert combat damage into cards and proliferate. The doublers are Hardened Scales, Kami of Whispered Hopes, The Earth Crystal, and Innkeeper's Talent at level 3. Your draw is Tidus's Cheer, Bred for the Hunt, Kutzil, Fathom Mage, Chasm Skulker and O'aka. Blitzball Stadium's Go for the Goal draws one card per *kind* of counter, so add kinds first with Arwen, Protection Magic or Elspeth's +1.

## Opening hands
- Keep three or more lands covering two colours, plus ramp or a cheap counter source.
- Ramp: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Farseek, Three Visits, Gyre Sage, Incubation Druid. Farseek finds Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista, Prairie Stream or Radiant Grove; Three Visits finds Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista or Radiant Grove.
- Count the lands that make G, W or U *on their own*. Flooded Grove, Forge of Heroes, Nesting Grounds, Overflowing Basin, Skycloud Expanse and Sungrass Prairie make no colour alone. A seven with one real colour, blue creatures and nothing under three mana does nothing until turn 3–4 — mulligan it while the mulligan is free (seen once).
- Many lands enter tapped. Plan the curve around them.

## Early turns and the commander
- Tidus only moves counters between creatures you control, and Cheer needs a creature with counters to connect.
- Land a counter source first: Shelinda, Scholar of New Horizons, Duskshell Crawler, Tromell or Innkeeper's Talent. Forge of Heroes gives Tidus a counter the turn he enters.
- Thrummingbird and Grateful Apparition proliferate early. Incubation Druid with a +1/+1 counter taps for three.
- Order beginning-of-combat triggers so Tidus's move resolves last.
- Tidus moves *any* counter, not just +1/+1: shift a stun counter off a creature you need onto one that isn't attacking. When Cheer proliferates, a chosen Saga also gains a lore counter — leave Sagas out unless you want the chapters to advance.

## How it wins
- **Evasion:** Herald of Secret Streams (unblockable with a +1/+1 counter), Rikku (unblockable whenever it gets counters), trample from Duskshell Crawler and Sphere Grid.
- **Multipliers:** Chocobo Knights (double strike), Wakka (counters on everything at end step), Tekuthal (proliferate twice), Inexorable Tide and Dreamtide Whale off spells.
- **Late:** Sin, Unending Cataclysm gathers your counters into one flying trampler. Walking Ballista finishes low totals. Fight Rigging frees its hidden card once you control power 7 or greater.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- **Board wipes are the real threat.** Hold Counterspell or An Offer You Can't Refuse. Ripples of Potential phases out what it proliferates — counters survive with the creature, and a creature *without* a counter is left behind. Inspiring Call and Protection Magic stop destruction and damage, not exile or bounce.
- **Your sweepers:** Damning Verdict is one-sided once every creature you control has a counter; fire it at lethal counterless attackers, even over a held trick, but check the life totals of the players you aren't racing (seen once). Promise of Loyalty and Farewell reset a losing board only.
- **Other removal:** Destroy Evil, Collective Effort, Summon: Ixion, Summon: Yojimbo — Yojimbo's chapter I exiles a *tapped* creature, so aim it at a commander that has already attacked, and chapters II–III tax attackers {2} while you rebuild (seen once).
- **Ignore** ground blockers once Herald is out. Cast Kutzil before a big turn: opponents can't cast spells during it.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Scattering counters.** Before combat, pile every source onto one evasive or trampling creature: The Ozolith, Fight Rigging, Innkeeper's Talent, Blitzball Stadium's support, Elspeth's +1, Tidus's move. Let Sagas and dying creatures feed The Ozolith. Then swing for the kill or for 21 commander damage.
- **Using removal on your own turn.** Keep one white open for Swords to Plowshares or Path to Exile, and answer an attacking commander *after* a pump like double strike is announced: the trick fizzles and the commander leaves.
- **Attacking a loaded Tidus into three or more untapped blockers** without evasion first. Gang blocks kill him and the counters are gone — Luminous Broodmoth returns him as a bare 3/3 with a flying counter (seen once).
- **Casting Farewell over your own board.** Check that The Ozolith or Resourceful Defense is out to catch the counters; otherwise you erase your investment and recast Tidus at +2 tax with nothing on him (seen once).
- **Paying high commander tax instead of solving the board.** Against fliers you can't block, deploy the flying bodies you already hold — Luminous Broodmoth, Grateful Apparition, Thrummingbird — before recasting a summoning-sick Tidus (seen once).
- **Missing the O'aka loop.** After a Summon Saga's chapter I resolves, tap O'aka to remove the lore counter and draw; chapter I triggers again next turn.
