# Tidus, Yuna's Guardian

## Gameplan
Stack +1/+1 counters on a few creatures, make them unblockable or trampling, and turn combat damage into cards and proliferate. The doublers are Hardened Scales, Kami of Whispered Hopes, The Earth Crystal, and Innkeeper's Talent at level 3. Your card draw is Tidus's Cheer, Bred for the Hunt, Kutzil, Fathom Mage, Chasm Skulker and O'aka. Blitzball Stadium's Go for the Goal draws one card per kind of counter, so add kinds first with Arwen, Protection Magic or Elspeth's +1.

## Opening hands
- Keep three or more lands covering two colours, plus ramp or a cheap counter piece.
- Ramp: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Farseek, Three Visits, Gyre Sage, Incubation Druid.
- Farseek finds Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista, Prairie Stream or Radiant Grove. Three Visits finds Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista or Radiant Grove.
- Count the lands that make G, W or U on their own (seen once). These make no colour alone: Flooded Grove, Forge of Heroes, Nesting Grounds, Overflowing Basin, Skycloud Expanse and Sungrass Prairie. With one real colour, blue creatures and nothing under three mana, the hand does nothing until turn 3–4. Mulligan it if the mulligan is free.
- Many lands enter tapped, so plan the curve around them.

## Early turns and the commander
- Tidus only moves counters between your own creatures. Cheer needs a creature with counters to connect.
- Before Tidus, cast a counter source: Shelinda, Scholar of New Horizons, Duskshell Crawler, Tromell or Innkeeper's Talent. Forge of Heroes gives Tidus a counter the turn he enters.
- Thrummingbird and Grateful Apparition proliferate early. Incubation Druid with a +1/+1 counter taps for three.
- Order your beginning-of-combat triggers so Tidus's move resolves last.

## How it wins
- **Evasion:** Herald of Secret Streams makes every creature with a +1/+1 counter unblockable. Rikku makes a creature unblockable whenever it gets counters. Duskshell Crawler and Sphere Grid give trample.
- **Multipliers:**
  - Chocobo Knights gives double strike to creatures with counters.
  - Wakka spreads counters at your end step.
  - Tekuthal doubles each proliferate.
  - Inexorable Tide and Dreamtide Whale proliferate off spells.
- **Late:** Sin, Unending Cataclysm gathers your counters into one flying trampler. Walking Ballista finishes low life totals. Fight Rigging casts its hidden card free once you control a creature with 7 or more power.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- **Board wipes are the real threat.** Hold Counterspell or An Offer You Can't Refuse.
  - Ripples of Potential phases out whatever it proliferates, but creatures without counters stay behind.
  - Inspiring Call and Protection Magic stop "destroy" and damage, not exile or bounce.
- **Your sweepers:** Damning Verdict is one-sided once all your creatures have counters. Use it against lethal counterless attackers (seen once). Farewell and Promise of Loyalty reset a losing board.
- **Other removal:** Destroy Evil, Collective Effort, Summon: Ixion, Summon: Yojimbo.
- **Ignore** ground blockers once Herald is out. Cast Kutzil before a big turn, because opponents can't cast spells during your turn.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Scattering counters.** Before combat, pile every source onto one evasive or trampling creature: The Ozolith, Fight Rigging, Innkeeper's Talent, Blitzball Stadium's support, Elspeth's +1, and Tidus's move. Let Sagas and creatures about to die hand their counters to The Ozolith. Then swing for the kill or for 21 commander damage.
- **Using removal on your own turn.** Keep one white open on opponents' turns for Swords to Plowshares or Path to Exile. Exile an attacking commander after a pump such as double strike is announced. Their trick fizzles and the commander leaves combat.
- **Forgetting Tidus moves any counter (seen once).** He can shift a stun counter onto a creature that isn't attacking.
- **Missing the O'aka loop (seen once).** After a Summon Saga's chapter I resolves, tap O'aka to remove the lore counter and draw. Chapter I then triggers again.
