# Tidus, Yuna's Guardian

## Gameplan
Put +1/+1 counters on a few creatures, make them hard to block, and turn combat damage into cards and proliferate. Doublers (Hardened Scales, Kami of Whispered Hopes, The Earth Crystal, Innkeeper's Talent at level 3) make every counter effect count for more. Cards come from Tidus's Cheer, Bred for the Hunt, Kutzil, Fathom Mage, Chasm Skulker and Blitzball Stadium's Go for the Goal. Close with one huge evasive threat or a wide pumped board.

## Opening hands
Keep hands with three or more lands that make two colors, plus ramp (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Farseek, Three Visits, Gyre Sage, Incubation Druid) or a cheap counter piece. Farseek can fetch Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista or Prairie Stream. Three Visits can fetch Breeding Pool, Canopy Vista or Radiant Grove. Many lands enter tapped, so plan your curve around them. Hands of only lands and six-drops are mulligans.

## Early turns and the commander
- Tidus moves counters only between two of your own creatures, and Cheer needs a creature with counters to connect. Have a counter source out before casting him: Shelinda, Scholar of New Horizons, Duskshell Crawler, Tromell or Innkeeper's Talent. Forge of Heroes gives Tidus a counter the turn he enters.
- Thrummingbird and Grateful Apparition proliferate early. Incubation Druid with any +1/+1 counter taps for three.
- Tidus, Maester Seymour, Kimahri, Fight Rigging, Innkeeper's Talent and The Ozolith all trigger at beginning of combat. Order them so Tidus's move resolves last.

## How it wins
- **Evasion:** Herald of Secret Streams makes every creature with a +1/+1 counter unblockable. Rikku does the same for one creature each time it gets counters. Duskshell Crawler and Sphere Grid give trample.
- **Multipliers:** Chocobo Knights gives double strike to every creature with counters. Wakka spreads counters across the team at end step.
- **Proliferate:** Tekuthal doubles it. Inexorable Tide and Dreamtide Whale proliferate off spells.
- **Late game:** Sin, Unending Cataclysm gathers your counters into one flying trampler. Walking Ballista finishes players at low life. Fight Rigging casts its hidden card free once you control a creature with 7 or more power.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- **Board wipes are the real threat.** Hold Counterspell or An Offer You Can't Refuse for them. Ripples of Potential phases out your counter-bearing permanents, which also dodges exile. Inspiring Call and Protection Magic stop "destroy" and damage, not exile.
- **Your sweepers:** Damning Verdict is one-sided when all your creatures have counters. Farewell and Promise of Loyalty reset a board you are losing on.
- **Spot removal:** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Destroy Evil, Collective Effort, Summon: Ixion, Summon: Yojimbo.
- **Ignore:** ground blockers once Herald is out. Cast Kutzil before a big turn, since opponents can't cast spells during your turn.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Scattering counters (seen once).** Pile counters onto one evasive or trampling creature before combat, then swing for lethal or 21 commander damage. Let creatures about to die hand their counters to The Ozolith.
- **Forgetting Tidus moves any counter (seen once).** Shift a stun counter off a creature you need.
- **Advancing Sagas by accident (seen once).** Proliferate adds lore counters. Leave Sagas out unless you want the next chapter.
- **Missing the O'aka and Valefor loop (seen once).** After Summon: Valefor's chapter I resolves, tap O'aka to remove its lore counter and draw. Chapter I triggers again at your next precombat main phase.
- **Using removal on your own turn (seen once).** Keep white open on opponents' turns. Exile an attacking commander after a pump is announced.
