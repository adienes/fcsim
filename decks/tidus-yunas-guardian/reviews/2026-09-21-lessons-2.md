# Lessons from 2026-09-21_173349_pod-the-wise-mothman-fire-lord-zuko-tidu_0041

- **interaction** Once an opponent has a flying commander you cannot block (you have no flier or reach creature in play), stop tapping out on your own turn. Pay with Forest, Hinterland Harbor and Exotic Orchard, and leave Canopy Vista, Fortified Village or Sunpetal Grove untapped for Swords to Plowshares or Path to Exile. Cut the least useful spell to do it, such as a level of Innkeeper's Talent. Use the removal the first time the commander attacks you. If you wait until its controller has green mana up and the hit would be lethal, a protection spell can blank your only answer. _Evidence: Game 0041. Path was in hand from round 2 and The Wise Mothman (a flier) landed on turn 16. On turn 18 Tidus tapped all five lands, including both white sources, and on turn 20 the Mothman hit for 9 commander damage while Path could not be cast. On turn 24 Path was cast into the Mothman's open Forest and Temple and met Heroic Intervention. The 17 damage that followed made 26 commander damage and knocked Tidus out._
- **sequencing** The counter-stacking plan works: put every counter onto Tidus himself. Move a counter from Shelinda with his combat trigger, add the Innkeeper's Talent counter (both doubled by The Earth Crystal), then use Blitzball Stadium's Go for the Goal to make him unblockable. Aim every hit at the same opponent until they reach 21 commander damage. Go for the Goal costs {3} and tapping the Stadium, so you get it once per turn. _Evidence: Game 0041. On turn 14 Tidus hit Fire Lord Zuko for 5. On turn 18 he had 8 counters and was unblockable, and he hit Zuko for 11, reaching 16 commander damage in two swings. The auditor flagged that the Stadium had not been tapped to pay for Go for the Goal._
