# Lessons from 2026-09-21_092534_pod-esika-tidus-fire-lord-zuko-kilo_0012, 2026-09-21_123655_pod-the-wise-mothman-edgar-markov-tidus-_0031

- **how_a_card_works** O'aka can pay its cost with a Saga's lore counter. Remove the only lore counter from Summon: Valefor after chapter I has resolved, and the Saga goes back to 1 at your next precombat main phase, so chapter I bounces each opponent's most expensive creature again. That costs a commander tempo twice for one card and draws you a card as well. _Evidence: Game 0031, turn 17: Valefor's chapter I sent all three commanders back to hand, and O'aka then drew Swords to Plowshares by removing the lore counter. On turn 21 chapter I fired again and bounced Mothman, Edgar and Party Thrasher. That cleared the skies for the 14-damage Valefor swing that knocked out Mothman._
- **sequencing** Before combat, pile every counter source onto one evasive or trampling creature: The Ozolith, Fight Rigging, Innkeeper's Talent, Blitzball Stadium's support, Elspeth's +1, and Tidus's own counter move. Let Sagas and creatures that are about to die put their counters on The Ozolith. Then swing for the kill or for 21 commander damage. _Evidence: Game 0031, turn 21: Valefor collected counters from Ozolith, Fight Rigging, Innkeeper's Talent, Stadium and Tidus and hit Mothman for 14, exactly lethal. On turn 27 Valefor was sacrificed at chapter IV and its 11 counters went onto The Ozolith, then onto Tidus. Tidus trampled 16 through two blockers onto Edgar, then hit Zuko for 25 commander damage on turn 29._
- **how_a_card_works** Use Tidus's beginning-of-combat move for more than +1/+1 counters: it can take a stun counter off a creature you need and put it on one that is not attacking. And when you choose a Saga for proliferate (Cheer), it must also get a lore counter, so leave the Saga out of the proliferate if you don't want its chapters to move on. _Evidence: Game 0031, turn 24: Zuko redirected Valefor's chapter II so it stunned Valefor itself, and Tidus's combat trigger moved that stun counter onto the freshly cast Kutzil. On the same turn the pilot proliferated Valefor's +1/+1 counters but skipped its lore counter. The auditor ruled it had to add the lore counter too._
- **interaction** Keep one white open for Swords to Plowshares or Path to Exile on opponents' turns, and use it on an attacking commander once a pump like double strike is announced, rather than on your own turn. Answering in response makes their trick fizzle and sends the commander back to the command zone. _Evidence: Game 0031, turn 26: Edgar Markov cast Boros Charm for double strike while attacking. With Boros Charm still on the stack, the pilot exiled Edgar with Swords from its held Plains. That stopped 12 commander damage, and the Charm lost its target._
