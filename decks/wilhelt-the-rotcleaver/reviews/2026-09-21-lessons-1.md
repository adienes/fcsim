# Lessons from 2026-09-20_184504_pod-fire-lord-zuko-lathril-miirym-wilhel_0006, 2026-09-20_222947_pod-fire-lord-zuko-lathril-miirym-wilhel_0007

- **opening_hand** Mulligan a seven with five or more lands and only one spell, and use the free first mulligan to do it. With 35 lands and several rocks this deck floods easily. It wants 3–4 lands with both colours, plus two or three plays at 1–3 mana (cheap Zombies, Skullclamp, a Signet) to feed Wilhelt's end-step sacrifice. _Evidence: Game 0007: the pilot kept Death Baron, Talisman and 5 lands with no mulligan. By round 6 it held 2 cards with 7 mana sources, and it did nothing on 3 of its 7 turns._
- **how_a_card_works** Wilhelt has two triggers you must claim yourself. First, at every end step where you control another Zombie, sacrifice a decayed or spare Zombie to draw; if the sacrificed Zombie didn't have decayed, you also get a decayed 2/2 back. Second, when a board wipe kills him along with other non-decayed Zombies, he still triggers for each of them and makes a decayed 2/2 per Zombie. _Evidence: Game 0006, round 7: Kindred Dominance destroyed Wilhelt and two Zombie tokens. The auditor had to add the two missed triggers, which made two decayed tokens. The round-8 end-step trigger was also skipped. Game 0007: the auditor flagged the missed end-step trigger in round 3, and the two dies-triggers for Death Baron and Zul Ashur after the round-4 Fireball, which were added late._
- **sequencing** Cast creatures, play lands and equip only in your own main phases with an empty stack. Equip Skullclamp before you cast Accursed Marauder, never in response to its trigger. Cast post-combat creatures such as Death Baron in main phase 2, not at end of combat. _Evidence: Game 0006: the round-1 Swamp played in upkeep was returned to hand and the land drop was lost. In round 4, equipping Skullclamp in response to the Marauder trigger was reversed and both drawn cards went back. In round 8, Death Baron cast at end of combat was rewound, so the seat had no deathtouch blockers going into Lathril's turn._
- **combat** Once Skullclamp is attached, the best use for decayed tokens is to equip one in your main phase, attack with the tokens, and let the end-of-combat sacrifice draw 2 cards off the clamp, plus a card per Zombie from Undead Augur. Attack at the player who is closest to winning. _Evidence: Game 0006, round 8: after Liliana's −3 brought Wilhelt back, the pilot clamped a decayed token and sent both tokens at Lathril for 7. The sacrifices drew four cards: Gleaming Overseer, Diregraf Colossus, Death Baron and Dimir Signet._
- **interaction** When your board is empty, keep Cyclonic Rift, Counterspell and An Offer You Can't Refuse for the spell that would kill you. Rift the creature a pump or fling spell is targeting, and counter the equipment or recast that follows. _Evidence: Game 0007, round 8: Zuko cast Chandra's Ignition on a 21-power Renegade Bull, which would have killed the pilot. Rift bounced the Bull so Ignition did nothing, and Counterspell stopped the recast. In round 9, An Offer countered Embercleave, which would have been 14 double-strike damage into the pilot's 7 life._
