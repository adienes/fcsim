# Lessons from 2026-09-21_182210_pod-miirym-obeka-wilhelt-fire-lord-zuko_0042

- **combat** A decayed token's best job is feeding Wilhelt's end-step sacrifice for a card. Only attack with it when no untapped creature can block it, or when a Skullclamp or death payoff makes the trade pay. Throwing it into an untapped blocker loses the card, and Wilhelt makes no token back. _Evidence: Game 0042, round 4: the pilot attacked Sarkhan Unbroken with its only decayed token while Miirym had an untapped 4/4 flying Dragon token. The Dragon blocked, the token died with no Wilhelt trigger, and at end step the pilot skipped Wilhelt's draw rather than sacrifice Cryptbreaker._
- **sequencing** Given 4+ black mana and Entomb, Victimize and a spare Zombie token: Entomb Gray Merchant of Asphodel, then Victimize it back along with Accursed Marauder or Fleshbag Marauder, sacrificing a token. Count devotion first. Warchief, Endless Ranks, Merchant and your one-pip Zombies add up fast. Finish with cheap removal on the last blocker and swing at the lowest-life player. _Evidence: Game 0042, round 6: Entomb put Gray Merchant in the graveyard. Victimize, paid with a plain token, returned Merchant and Accursed Marauder, and Merchant drained each opponent for 9 (19 to 46 life). The Marauder edict made Zuko give up his commander. Feed the Swarm killed Avatar Roku, and four attackers dealt 15 to knock Zuko out from 14._
- **how_a_card_works** When Accursed Marauder resolves and your other nontoken creatures matter, sacrifice the Marauder itself. Wilhelt still gives you a decayed 2/2, and you should cast it when an opponent's only nontoken creature is their commander. Name every opponent's options out loud so they choose on the record. _Evidence: Game 0042, round 5: the pilot cast Marauder for {B} under Undead Warchief and sacrificed Marauder to its own trigger. Miirym had to sacrifice Miirym, her only nontoken creature, and Wilhelt made a decayed token. In round 6 a returned Marauder took Zuko's commander, Thunderbreak Regent and Ganax._
- **how_a_card_works** Endless Ranks of the Dead, Diregraf Colossus and Cryptbreaker make plain 2/2 Zombies that can block. Only Wilhelt's tokens have decayed. When you sacrifice a non-decayed Zombie at Wilhelt's end step, the decayed replacement keeps your Zombie count, and so your next Endless Ranks trigger, the same size. _Evidence: Game 0042, round 5: the table marked an Endless Ranks token as decayed, and the auditor corrected it. The round-7 audit found the same error on Endless Ranks, Colossus and Cryptbreaker tokens. In round 6 the pilot sacrificed a plain token at end step and noted that its Zombie count stayed at 8 for Endless Ranks._
- **combat** Pay Cryptbreaker's 'tap three Zombies' cost with decayed tokens (which can't block anyway) and summoning-sick creatures first. Keep at least one plain Zombie untapped as a chump blocker against an opponent whose commander grows when it attacks. _Evidence: Game 0042, round 5: the pilot tapped Cryptbreaker, its Endless Ranks token and Undead Warchief to draw one card, then attacked with Wilhelt, leaving no creature untapped.
