# Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver

## Gameplan
This is Zombie aristocrats. Go wide, sacrifice Zombies for cards and drain, and rebuild from the graveyard. Wilhelt makes a decayed 2/2 whenever another non-decayed Zombie you control dies. At your end step, he lets you sacrifice a Zombie to draw.

- **Outlets:** Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar, Carrion Feeder.
- **Death payoffs:** Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead, Plague Belcher, Undead Augur, Midnight Reaper, Headless Rider, Grave Pact.
- **Rebuilding:** Gisa and Geralf, Zul Ashur, Lich Lord, Tomb Tyrant, Liliana, Death's Majesty, Zombie Apocalypse.

## Opening hands
- The deck runs 35 lands plus rocks, so it floods.
- **Mulligan** a seven with five or more lands and one spell. Use the free first mulligan for it (seen once).
- **Keep** three or four lands covering both colours, plus two or three plays at 1–3 mana: Carrion Feeder, Cryptbreaker, Champion of the Perished, Gravecrawler, Skullclamp, a Signet.
- Skullclamp plus any cheap Zombie is a keep by itself.

## Early turns and the commander
- Before Wilhelt, lead with cheap Zombies and rocks. Undead Warchief discounts the rest.
- At end step, offer a decayed token or a spare Zombie. A non-decayed Zombie also gives back a decayed 2/2.
- **With Skullclamp:** equip a decayed token in your main phase, then attack with the decayed tokens. The end-of-combat sacrifice draws two. Undead Augur adds one card per Zombie. Attack the player closest to winning.
- **Accursed Marauder:** sacrifice the Marauder itself to spare your nontoken creatures. It is best when an opponent's only nontoken creature is their commander (seen once).
- **Cryptbreaker:** pay its tap cost with decayed tokens and summoning-sick Zombies (seen once).

## How it wins
1. **Gravecrawler loop.** Needs Gravecrawler, Phyrexian Altar and another Zombie. Sacrifice Gravecrawler for {B}, then recast it from the graveyard. Each loop drains through Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead or Plague Belcher, makes a decayed 2/2 and draws off Undead Augur. Watch Augur's life loss.
2. **Rooftop Storm.** It makes Gravecrawler free, so Carrion Feeder or Ashnod's Altar gives unlimited deaths. Ashnod's Altar also gives unlimited colourless mana.
3. **Wide board.** Army of the Damned or Endless Ranks of the Dead, plus lords, is lethal.
   - Lords: Cemetery Reaper, Death Baron, Lord of the Accursed, Lord of the Undead, Tomb Tyrant, Undead Warchief, Liliana's Mastery.
   - Evasion: Hordewing Skaab, Zombie Master, Lord of the Accursed.
   - An overloaded Cyclonic Rift clears the blockers.
4. **Drain.** Gray Merchant of Asphodel drains for your devotion to black.
   - With four or more black mana, Entomb Merchant.
   - Then Victimize it back alongside a Marauder, sacrificing a token.
   - Count devotion first, then swing at the lowest life total (seen once).

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer:**
- Graveyard hate.
- Anything that stops creatures dying or entering.
- Opposing combo pieces. Use Counterspell, Negate or An Offer You Can't Refuse on the noncreature ones.

Save Toxic Deluge for boards you can't outgrow. Your Zombies come back.

**Ignore** small creatures. Grave Pact, both Marauders, Noxious Ghoul and deathtouch from Death Baron or Diregraf Captain grind them out.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Skipping Wilhelt's end-step sacrifice.** It is a free card each turn, but only if you choose to sacrifice.
- **Forgetting that wipes still pay.** If a wipe kills Wilhelt along with other non-decayed Zombies, he still triggers for each of them.
- **Equipping Skullclamp in response to Accursed Marauder's trigger.** Equip is sorcery-speed, so clamp the creature you'll give up first (seen once).
- **Acting at odd timings.** Cast creatures, play lands and equip in your main phases on an empty stack (seen once).
- **Spending counters and Cyclonic Rift early.** Hold them for the spell that would kill you (seen once).
