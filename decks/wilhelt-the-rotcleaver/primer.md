# Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver

## Gameplan
This is Zombie aristocrats. Go wide, sacrifice Zombies for cards and drain, then rebuild from the graveyard. Wilhelt makes a decayed 2/2 whenever another non-decayed Zombie you control dies. At your end step, he lets you sacrifice a Zombie to draw.

- **Outlets:** Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar, Carrion Feeder.
- **Death payoffs:** Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead, Plague Belcher, Undead Augur, Midnight Reaper, Headless Rider, Grave Pact.
- **Rebuilding:** Gisa and Geralf, Zul Ashur, Lich Lord, Tomb Tyrant, Liliana, Death's Majesty, Zombie Apocalypse.

## Opening hands
- The deck runs 35 lands plus rocks, so it floods.
- **Mulligan** a seven with five or more lands and one spell. Use the free first mulligan for it (seen once).
- **Keep** three or four lands covering both colours, plus two or three plays at 1–3 mana. Good examples are Carrion Feeder, Cryptbreaker, Champion of the Perished, Gravecrawler, Skullclamp and a Signet.
- Skullclamp plus any cheap Zombie is a keep by itself.

## Early turns and the commander
- Before Wilhelt, lead with cheap Zombies and rocks. Undead Warchief discounts the rest.
- **Skullclamp:** equip a decayed token in your main phase, then attack with the decayed tokens. The end-of-combat sacrifice draws two, and Undead Augur adds one card per Zombie. Attack the player closest to winning. Clamping an X/1 Zombie such as Gravecrawler kills it at once, for two cards and a decayed 2/2.
- **Accursed Marauder:** sacrifice the Marauder itself to spare your nontoken creatures, and Wilhelt still gives you a 2/2. It is best when an opponent's only nontoken creature is their commander.
- **Entomb Gravecrawler** at an opponent's end step on turn 1 or 2. After Wilhelt lands, recast it for {B} and feed it to him each end step (seen once).

## How it wins
1. **Gravecrawler loop.** You need Gravecrawler, Phyrexian Altar and another Zombie. Sacrifice Gravecrawler for {B}, then recast it from the graveyard. Each loop drains through Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead or Plague Belcher, makes a decayed 2/2 and draws off Undead Augur. Watch the life you lose to Augur.
2. **Rooftop Storm.** It makes Gravecrawler free, so Carrion Feeder or Ashnod's Altar gives unlimited deaths. Ashnod's Altar also gives unlimited colourless mana.
3. **Wide board.** Army of the Damned or Endless Ranks of the Dead, plus lords, is lethal.
   - Lords: Cemetery Reaper, Death Baron, Lord of the Accursed, Lord of the Undead, Tomb Tyrant, Undead Warchief, Liliana's Mastery.
   - Evasion: Hordewing Skaab, Zombie Master, Lord of the Accursed, Gleaming Overseer.
   - An overloaded Cyclonic Rift clears the blockers.
4. **Grave Pact grind.** Each turn, recast Gravecrawler and sacrifice it at end step. With a decayed token's attack, that is two edicts for {B} (seen once).
5. **Gray Merchant of Asphodel** drains for your devotion to black. Entomb it, then Victimize it back (seen once).

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer** graveyard hate, anything that stops creatures dying or entering, and opposing combo pieces. Use Counterspell, Negate or An Offer You Can't Refuse on the noncreature ones. Save Toxic Deluge for boards you can't outgrow, because your Zombies come back.

**Ignore** small creatures. Grave Pact, both Marauders, Noxious Ghoul and deathtouch from Death Baron or Diregraf Captain grind them out.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Skipping Wilhelt's end-step sacrifice.** It is a free card every turn, but only if you choose to sacrifice. Offer a decayed token or a spare Zombie.
- **Forgetting that wipes still pay.** If a wipe kills Wilhelt along with other non-decayed Zombies, he still makes a decayed 2/2 for each of them.
- **Equipping Skullclamp in response to Marauder.** Equip is sorcery-speed, so clamp first (seen once).
- **Acting at odd timings.** Cast creatures and equip in main phases on an empty stack (seen once).
- **Spending counters and Rift early.** Hold them for the spell that would kill you (seen once).
