# Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver

## Gameplan
This is Zombie aristocrats: go wide, sacrifice Zombies for cards and drain, and rebuild from the graveyard. Wilhelt makes a decayed 2/2 whenever another non-decayed Zombie you control dies. At your end step, he lets you sacrifice a Zombie to draw a card.

- **Sacrifice outlets:** Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar, Carrion Feeder.
- **Payoffs when Zombies die:** Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead, Plague Belcher, Undead Augur, Midnight Reaper, Grave Pact.
- **Refilling the board:** Gisa and Geralf, Zul Ashur, Lich Lord, Tomb Tyrant, Liliana, Death's Majesty, Zombie Apocalypse.

## Opening hands
- The deck runs 35 lands plus rocks, so it floods easily.
- **Mulligan** a seven with five or more lands and one spell. Use the free first mulligan to do it (seen once).
- **Keep** three or four lands that cover both colours, plus two or three plays at 1–3 mana: Carrion Feeder, Cryptbreaker, Champion of the Perished, Gravecrawler, Skullclamp, a Signet.
- Skullclamp with any cheap Zombie is a keep on its own.

## Early turns and the commander
- Before Wilhelt, lead with cheap Zombies and rocks. Undead Warchief makes your other Zombies cheaper.
- For the end-step trigger, sacrifice a decayed token or a spare Zombie. A non-decayed Zombie also returns a decayed 2/2.
- With Skullclamp out, clamp a decayed token and attack with your decayed tokens. The end-of-combat sacrifice draws two off the clamp, plus one per Zombie from Undead Augur. Attack the player closest to winning (seen once).

## How it wins
1. **Gravecrawler loop:** you need Gravecrawler, Phyrexian Altar and another Zombie. Sacrifice Gravecrawler for {B}, then recast it from the graveyard for {B}. Each loop drains with Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead or Plague Belcher, makes a decayed 2/2 and draws with Undead Augur.
2. **Rooftop Storm:** it makes Gravecrawler free. Any free outlet (Ashnod's Altar, Carrion Feeder) then gives unlimited deaths. Ashnod's Altar also gives unlimited colourless mana.
3. **Wide board:** Army of the Damned or Endless Ranks of the Dead, plus lords, makes a lethal board.
   - Lords: Cemetery Reaper, Death Baron, Lord of the Accursed, Lord of the Undead, Tomb Tyrant, Undead Warchief, Liliana's Mastery.
   - Evasion: Hordewing Skaab (flying), Zombie Master (swampwalk), Lord of the Accursed (menace).
   - Overloaded Cyclonic Rift clears blockers.
4. **Drain:** Gray Merchant of Asphodel drains for your devotion to black. Grave Pact plus mass sacrifice strips boards before the swing.

Entomb, Reanimate and Victimize fetch or return key pieces.

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer:**
- Graveyard hate: Rest in Peace effects and graveyard exile.
- Anything that stops creatures dying or entering.
- Opposing combo pieces. Use Counterspell, Negate or An Offer You Can't Refuse on the noncreature ones.

Save Toxic Deluge for boards you can't outgrow. Your Zombies come back, and Wilhelt refunds tokens.

**Ignore** small creatures. Grave Pact, Fleshbag Marauder, Accursed Marauder, Noxious Ghoul and deathtouch from Death Baron or Diregraf Captain grind them out.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Skipping Wilhelt's end-step sacrifice.** It is a free card every turn. It fires only at your own end step, and only if you choose to sacrifice.
- **Forgetting that wipes still pay.** If a wipe kills Wilhelt along with other non-decayed Zombies, he triggers for each of them. You keep a decayed 2/2 per Zombie.
- **Trying to equip Skullclamp in response to Accursed Marauder's trigger.** Equipping is sorcery-speed. Equip the creature you'll give up before you cast Marauder.
- **Casting creatures or equipping at odd timings.** Do it in main phases on an empty stack. Death Baron goes down in main phase 2.
- **Spending counters and Cyclonic Rift early.** With an empty board, hold Cyclonic Rift, Counterspell and An Offer You Can't Refuse for the spell that would kill you. Rift the creature a pump or fling spell targets. Counter the equipment or recast that follows.
- **Keeping land-heavy sevens** instead of taking the free mulligan (seen once).
