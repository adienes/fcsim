# Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver

## Gameplan
Zombie aristocrats. Go wide, sacrifice Zombies for cards and drain, rebuild from the graveyard. Wilhelt makes a decayed 2/2 whenever another non-decayed Zombie you control dies, and at your end step he turns a Zombie into a card.

- **Outlets:** Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar, Carrion Feeder, Cleaver Skaab.
- **Death payoffs:** Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead, Plague Belcher, Undead Augur, Midnight Reaper, Headless Rider, Grave Pact.
- **Rebuilding:** Gisa and Geralf, Zul Ashur, Lich Lord, Tomb Tyrant, Unholy Grotto, Liliana, Death's Majesty, Zombie Apocalypse.

## Opening hands
- 35 lands plus rocks means this deck floods. Mulligan a seven with five or more lands and one spell; the free first mulligan is there for exactly this.
- Keep three or four lands covering both colours plus two or three plays at 1–3 mana: Carrion Feeder, Cryptbreaker, Champion of the Perished, Gravecrawler, Skullclamp, a Signet.
- Skullclamp with any cheap Zombie is a keep by itself.

## Early turns and the commander
- Lead with cheap Zombies and rocks. Undead Warchief discounts everything after it.
- **Entomb Gravecrawler** at an opponent's end step on turn 1 or 2 if the mana would go unused. Cast Wilhelt, recast Gravecrawler for {B}, and feed it to him every end step thereafter — a free card per turn forever.
- **Wilhelt's end step is yours to claim.** It is optional: say it every turn. Sacrificing a non-decayed Zombie also hands back a decayed 2/2.
- **Skullclamp:** equip a decayed token in your main phase, attack with the tokens, and the end-of-combat sacrifice draws two, plus one card per Zombie from Undead Augur. Clamping an X/1 such as Gravecrawler kills it immediately for two cards and a decayed 2/2. Attack the player closest to winning.
- **Accursed Marauder:** sacrifice the Marauder itself to spare your nontoken creatures — Wilhelt still pays you. Best when an opponent's only nontoken creature is their commander.
- Pay Cryptbreaker's "tap three Zombies" with decayed tokens and summoning-sick creatures; keep one real Zombie back as a blocker (seen once).

## How it wins
1. **Gravecrawler loop.** Gravecrawler, Phyrexian Altar, another Zombie: sacrifice for {B}, recast, repeat. Each iteration drains through Diregraf Captain, Vengeful Dead or Plague Belcher, makes a decayed 2/2, and draws off Undead Augur. Watch the life Augur costs you.
2. **Rooftop Storm** makes Gravecrawler free; Carrion Feeder or Ashnod's Altar then gives unlimited deaths, and the Altar unlimited mana.
3. **Wide board.** Army of the Damned or Endless Ranks of the Dead plus lords — Cemetery Reaper, Death Baron, Lord of the Accursed, Lord of the Undead, Tomb Tyrant, Undead Warchief, Liliana's Mastery. Evasion from Hordewing Skaab, Zombie Master, Gleaming Overseer, or an overloaded Cyclonic Rift.
4. **Grave Pact grind.** Recast Gravecrawler for {B}, attack with a decayed token so its end-of-combat sacrifice triggers Pact, then feed Gravecrawler to Wilhelt: two edicts a turn for one mana (seen once).
5. **Gray Merchant of Asphodel.** Count devotion first; Entomb him and Victimize him back alongside a Marauder (seen once).

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer** graveyard hate, anything stopping creatures from dying or entering, and opposing combo pieces — Counterspell, Negate, An Offer You Can't Refuse for the noncreature half. With an empty board, hold Rift and counters for the spell that kills you. Save Toxic Deluge for boards you cannot outgrow; your Zombies return, theirs do not.

**Ignore** small creatures. Grave Pact, both Marauders, Noxious Ghoul and deathtouch grind them down.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Missing that wipes still pay.** A wipe killing Wilhelt alongside other non-decayed Zombies still triggers for each of them.
- **Losing Wilhelt to exile.** When he would go to the graveyard or exile, you may move him to the command zone instead — take it unless you genuinely have Unholy Grotto, Relentless Dead or Zombie Apocalypse ready.
- **Odd timings.** Cast creatures and equip in your main phases on an empty stack. Clamp before casting Accursed Marauder, never in response to its trigger.
- **Attacking after the fact.** Wilhelt's end step is post-combat, so send the Zombie you plan to eat in first, at whoever has no untapped blocker.
- **Rifting too cheaply.** A single Rift on a countered-up opposing commander mid-combat strips its counters and its tempo (seen once).
