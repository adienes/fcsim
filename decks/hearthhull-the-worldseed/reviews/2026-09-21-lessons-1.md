# Lessons from 2026-09-21_104400_pod-hearthhull-kuja-omnath-fire-lord-zuk_0021, 2026-09-21_124814_pod-fire-lord-zuko-terra-hearthhull-zaxa_0033

- **sequencing** Hearthhull does nothing until another creature taps to station it, so have a creature in play the turn before you cast it. Horizon Explorer adds only 2 counters. The Beast tokens from Baloth Prime add 4 each, and Baloth Prime itself adds 10. Cast Baloth Prime and sacrifice a land the same turn to start the Beasts coming, then get Hearthhull to 8 counters (flying, vigilance, haste) as fast as you can. _Evidence: Game 1: only Horizon Explorer was stationing, so Hearthhull sat at 2 and then 4 counters from rounds 4 to 5 and never attacked. Game 2, round 4: Baloth Prime came down and Escape Tunnel was sacrificed for a 4/4 Beast. In round 5 a Beast put Hearthhull straight to 4. In round 6 a second Beast took it to 8, and Hearthhull, Broodship and Explorer attacked for 12. In round 8 Baloth Prime alone stationed it to 10._
- **commander_timing** After Hearthhull is recast, tap two 4-power Beasts to station it: it goes straight back to 8 counters and has haste, so the drain and draw work that same turn. Removal on Hearthhull costs you only the commander tax, as long as you keep two untapped 4-power creatures for it. _Evidence: Game 2, round 9: The Kami War had exiled Hearthhull. It was recast for 8, two Beasts put it to 8 counters, and it sacrificed Stomping Ground at once for the drain that took Terra from 1 to -1._
- **interaction** Save Rakdos Charm's graveyard-exile mode for effects that bring back a whole graveyard, such as a saga or spell that returns all lands. Do not spend it stopping one flashback or one reanimation target. It is also the deck's cheap instant answer to an artifact, so keep it for either job. _Evidence: Game 2, round 5 (turn 19): it answered Summon: Titan's chapter II and exiled 12 cards from Terra's graveyard, 7 of them lands with four fetchlands among them, before they could come back. Game 1, round 3 (turn 11): the Charm only stopped Kuja's Flashback on a single Dark Ritual. Nothing was left to answer Kuja's artifacts or board later, and Kuja won._
- **interaction** Do not aim Putrefy or other destroy removal at a creature whose indestructibility depends on something the opponent can change at instant speed. Ozai, the Phoenix King is indestructible at 6+ unspent mana, and its controller can tap a land in response to reach 6. Use exile instead (Tear Asunder kicked), or cast destroy only when they cannot reach the threshold even by tapping everything. _Evidence: Game 2, round 7 (turn 25): Putrefy targeted Ozai while Zuko had 5 unspent mana. Zuko tapped a Mountain in response, reached 6, Ozai became indestructible and the Putrefy was wasted._
