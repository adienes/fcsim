# Lessons from 2026-09-21_201717_pod-wilhelt-obeka-hearthhull-fire-lord-z_0051

- **commander_timing** When Hearthhull is destroyed or dies, move it to the command zone right away, at the moment it hits the graveyard. If you leave it there, you cannot cast it again without a recursion card. Recasting it for {2} more and restationing it with untapped creatures is the whole plan after removal. _Evidence: Game 0051, round 6 (table turn 23): Feed the Swarm destroyed Hearthhull at 8 counters, and Wilhelt even told the pilot it could go to the command zone. The pilot left it in the graveyard. Its notes on rounds 7, 9 and 10 say 'Hearthhull is stuck in the graveyard, so I can't cast it again from the command zone' and 'no recursion yet'. The deck played its last five rounds without its commander, while Rampaging Baloths, a Beast token and Augur of Autumn were in play and could have restationed it._
- **sequencing** You can station Hearthhull with a swarm of small creatures as well as with big ones. Scute Swarm, plus a fetch land or crack-land played the same turn, gives you several 1-power bodies. Hold Hearthhull until you can tap about 4 power at once. Then put each later creature, even a summoning-sick one like Evendo Brushrazer, onto it the turn it arrives. _Evidence: Game 0051: on round 3 the pilot cast Scute Swarm, then played and cracked Escape Tunnel for two Insects. On round 4 Myriad Landscape added a third, and it cast Hearthhull and stationed it to 4 with the Swarm and three Insects. Mazirek took it to 6 on round 5 and a freshly cast Brushrazer took it to 8 on round 6. It attacked for 6 with haste that same turn._
- **interaction** Against a graveyard-recursion deck, hold Rakdos Charm's exile mode for the turn their graveyard holds the engine. Cast it right after a sweeper has filled that graveyard with their best creatures, so it exiles them all at once. Planetary Annihilation followed by Rakdos Charm in the same main phase does exactly this. _Evidence: Game 0051: Rakdos Charm was held open from round 6 on against Wilhelt's Zombie Apocalypse. On round 10 Planetary Annihilation killed four Zombies (22 power), and Wilhelt had no mana left to return Relentless Dead. Rakdos Charm then exiled Wilhelt's 12-card graveyard, including Zombie Apocalypse, Gravecrawler and the dead Zombies._
- **how_a_card_works** Mazirek, Evendo Brushrazer and Hearthhull's drain trigger only on sacrifices. A creature destroyed by a wrath, by removal or by lethal damage does not count. Do not put their triggers on the stack for deaths that were not sacrifices, and do not count on them against a destroy-based sweeper. Grave Pact, Fleshbag Marauder and your own land sacrifices do count. _Evidence: Game 0051, round 5 (table turn 19): the pilot put two Mazirek triggers on the stack when Zombie Apocalypse destroyed Ozai and Generous Plunderer. The auditor ruled them illegal. On table turn 22, Brushrazer was destroyed by Chandra's Ignition damage and correctly did not trigger._
- **sequencing** Keep Worldsoul's Rage until X can kill a player outright. Its X also brings back up to X lands from your graveyard, so the same spell rebuilds your mana base after land sacrifices. _Evidence: Game 0051: on round 8 the pilot held Rage with Obeka at 11. On round 9, with Obeka at 5, it cast X=5 from 7 lands. That knocked Obeka out and returned Eumidian Hatchery, Festering Thicket and Escape Tunnel, taking the pilot from 7 lands to 10._
