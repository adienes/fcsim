# Lessons from 2026-09-21_173349_pod-the-wise-mothman-fire-lord-zuko-tidu_0041

- **how_a_card_works** Once Hearthhull has 8 charge counters, Sylvan Safekeeper (and Zuran Orb) are free sacrifice outlets. Each land you sacrifice to them makes every opponent lose 2, at no mana cost and even at instant speed. Before you tap lands for spells, count your lands times 2 against each opponent's life. The whole mana base can be a lethal drain. _Evidence: Game 0041, round 7 (table turn 26): Hearthhull was at 8 counters. The pilot sacrificed five lands to Sylvan Safekeeper's shroud ability, with no mana spent. That was 10 life from each opponent: The Wise Mothman at 7 and Fire Lord Zuko at 9 both died, and it won the game._
- **sequencing** Station counts power, so several small creatures tapped together add up. Before stationing, pump one with Szarel: each land you sacrifice during your turn puts counters equal to Szarel's power (2) on another creature, and those count toward the 8. Make sure the total reaches 8 in one turn, so Hearthhull flies, has haste and drains at once. _Evidence: Game 0041, round 6 (table turn 23): the pilot cast Hearthhull and Safekeeper, then sacrificed a Forest to Safekeeper. Szarel's trigger made Tireless Provisioner 5 power. Tapping Provisioner (5), Szarel (2) and Safekeeper (1) gave exactly 8 counters. Without the Szarel counters it would have had only 6. Hearthhull then attacked for 6 that turn._
- **interaction** Aim Putrefy at an opponent's counter-stacked commander on your own main phase, when its controller is tapped out and before they can give it ward or protection. Holding Putrefy open for several rounds is fine, but fire it once one creature threatens a commander-damage kill. _Evidence: Game 0041, round 5 (table turn 19): Tidus, Yuna's Guardian was 13/13 with 10 counters, had already dealt Zuko 16 commander damage, and Innkeeper's Talent could reach level 2 (ward). Putrefy killed Tidus while its controller had no mana open, and the counters were lost when it went to the command zone. Tidus had to recast it from zero._
