# Hearthhull, the Worldseed

## Gameplan
This deck turns lands into damage, cards and bodies.
- **Extra land drops:** Azusa, Lost but Seeking, Exploration, Oracle of Mul Daya, Icetill Explorer, The Gitrog Monster and Exploration Broodship.
- **Land sacrifice:** fetches, Crop Rotation, Harrow and Zuran Orb.
- **Replaying lands from the graveyard:** Ramunap Excavator, Szarel, Genesis Shepherd, Conduit of Worlds and Walk-In Closet.
- **Landfall payoffs:** Omnath, Locus of Rage, Rampaging Baloths, Scute Swarm, Tannuk, Memorial Ensign and Lotus Cobra.
- **Sacrifice payoffs:** Hearthhull, Korvold, Fae-Cursed King, Mayhem Devil, Titania, Protector of Argoth, Baloth Prime and Scouring Swarm.

A fetchland you replay from the graveyard fires both landfall and sacrifice triggers.

## Opening hands
Keep hands with three or more lands and a ramp spell or a green two- or three-drop. Hands heavy on lands are fine, because this deck spends lands as a resource. Mulligan hands that can't make green early; most of the engine is green. Fetches and the Overlook-style lands count as colour fixing, not just land drops.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp first. Hearthhull does nothing alone. To station it, you tap another untapped creature, and only at sorcery speed. Have a creature in play the turn before you cast it.

At 2 or more counters Hearthhull draws: {1}, tap, sacrifice a land to draw two and make an extra land drop. At 8 counters it becomes a hasty flyer, and each land you sacrifice drains each opponent for 2. Reaching 8 is the turning point. Station adds counters equal to the tapped creature's power:
- Two 4-power Beast tokens reach 8 at once.
- Horizon Explorer adds only 2.

Baloth Prime is Hearthhull's best partner, but it is slow. It enters tapped with six stun counters, and its Beast tokens also enter tapped. Neither can station until it untaps. Once untapped, Baloth Prime alone adds 10.

## How it wins
- With Hearthhull at 8, sacrifice lands in bulk with Scapeshift, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth and replayed fetches. Mayhem Devil or Korvold add a ping or a card per sacrifice. The Gitrog Monster draws whenever lands hit your graveyard.
- Scapeshift or Splendid Reclamation with Omnath or Rampaging Baloths builds a board of tokens in one turn. Moraug, Fury of Akoum turns each main-phase land drop into an extra combat.
- Worldsoul's Rage and Valakut Exploration finish opponents.
- Planetary Annihilation deals 6 to each creature. Hearthhull and Baloth Prime have 7 or more toughness and survive it. The lands it sacrifices fire every sacrifice trigger you have.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- Answer graveyard hate and anything that stops sacrifices or landfall.
- Answer flyers and fast combo with Infernal Grasp, Putrefy, Beast Within and Tear Asunder. Kicked Tear Asunder exiles.
- Rakdos Charm is your cheap instant answer to an artifact. Pest Infestation handles several artifacts or enchantments at once.
- Blasphemous Act kills your engine creatures too; cast it only when behind.
- Race slow, fair creature decks with drain instead of spending removal on them.
- Sylvan Safekeeper protects a key creature from targeted removal.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- Casting Hearthhull with no untapped creature to station it (seen once). Build toward 8 counters in a single station.
- Holding Hearthhull back for fear of removal (seen once). It has haste. Recast it with two untapped 4-power creatures and it drains and draws that same turn. Removal costs you only the commander tax.
- Spending Rakdos Charm's graveyard-exile mode on a single flashback or reanimation target (seen once). Save it, or Bojuka Bog, for effects that return a whole graveyard.
- Aiming destroy effects at a creature the opponent can make indestructible at instant speed (seen once). Use kicked Tear Asunder, or wait until they can't.
