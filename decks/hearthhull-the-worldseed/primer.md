# Hearthhull, the Worldseed

## Gameplan
Lands are your resource. Play extra lands, sacrifice them and replay them, and turn every step into damage, cards and bodies.
- **Extra land drops:** Azusa, Lost but Seeking, Exploration, Oracle of Mul Daya, Icetill Explorer, The Gitrog Monster, Exploration Broodship.
- **Land sacrifice:** fetches, Crop Rotation, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth, Zuran Orb, Sylvan Safekeeper.
- **Lands from the graveyard:** Ramunap Excavator, Szarel, Genesis Shepherd, Conduit of Worlds, Walk-In Closet, Icetill Explorer.
- **Landfall payoffs:** Omnath, Locus of Rage, Rampaging Baloths, Scute Swarm, Tannuk, Memorial Ensign, Lotus Cobra.
- **Sacrifice payoffs:** Hearthhull, Korvold, Fae-Cursed King, Mayhem Devil, Titania, Protector of Argoth, Baloth Prime, Scouring Swarm.

A replayed fetchland fires both landfall and sacrifice triggers.

## Opening hands
Keep three or more lands plus a ramp spell or a green two- or three-drop. Land-heavy hands are fine. Mulligan hands that can't make green early. Fetches and the Overlook-style lands count as fixing.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp first, because Hearthhull does nothing alone. Station taps another creature at sorcery speed only, so have one in play the turn before you cast it. Station adds counters equal to that creature's power: Horizon Explorer adds 2, each Baloth Prime Beast adds 4 and Baloth Prime adds 10.

At 2 counters, pay {1}, tap Hearthhull and sacrifice a land to draw two and get an extra land drop. At 8 it gains flying, vigilance and haste, and every land you sacrifice drains each opponent for 2. Reach 8 fast.

Baloth Prime enters tapped with six stun counters. Sacrifice a land the turn you cast it. Each sacrifice starts a Beast coming and removes a stun counter. The Beasts enter tapped, so they can station the next turn.

## How it wins
- **Drain with Hearthhull at 8.** Sacrifice lands in bulk with Scapeshift, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth and replayed fetches. Mayhem Devil adds a ping per sacrifice and Korvold adds a card. The Gitrog Monster draws as lands hit the graveyard.
- **Count for lethal (seen once).** Zuran Orb and Sylvan Safekeeper sacrifice for free at instant speed. Before you tap lands for spells, compare twice your land count with each opponent's life.
- **Build a board.** Scapeshift or Splendid Reclamation with Omnath or Rampaging Baloths makes a board of tokens in one turn. Moraug, Fury of Akoum turns each main-phase land drop into an extra combat.
- **Finish** with Valakut Exploration or Worldsoul's Rage. Hold the Rage until X kills a player (seen once).
- **Planetary Annihilation** deals 6 to each creature. Hearthhull at 8 and Baloth Prime survive it, and its land sacrifices fire your triggers.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- Answer graveyard hate and anything that stops sacrifices or landfall.
- Answer flyers and fast combo with Infernal Grasp, Putrefy, Beast Within and Tear Asunder. Kicked Tear Asunder exiles.
- Rakdos Charm is your cheap instant-speed artifact answer. Pest Infestation handles several artifacts or enchantments.
- Blasphemous Act kills your engine too, so cast it only when behind.
- Race slow, fair creature decks with drain rather than spending removal on them.
- Sylvan Safekeeper protects a key creature from targeted removal.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Casting Hearthhull with no untapped creature to station it.** Aim for 8 counters in one station.
- **Spending Rakdos Charm's graveyard exile on one flashback or reanimation target.** Save it, or Bojuka Bog, for effects that return a whole graveyard, such as mass land returns.
- **Fearing removal on Hearthhull (seen once).** Send it to the command zone when it dies, then recast it and restation it. Two untapped Beasts return it straight to 8 with haste.
- **Destroying a creature its controller can make indestructible at instant speed (seen once).** Use kicked Tear Asunder instead, or wait until they can't pay for the protection.
- **Counting deaths as sacrifices (seen once).** Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest and Evendo Brushrazer ignore creatures that die to removal, wraths or damage.
