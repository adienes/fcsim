# Hearthhull, the Worldseed

## Gameplan
This deck turns lands into damage, cards and bodies.
- **Extra land drops:** Azusa, Lost but Seeking, Exploration, Oracle of Mul Daya, Icetill Explorer, The Gitrog Monster and Exploration Broodship.
- **Land sacrifice:** fetches, Crop Rotation, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth, Zuran Orb and Sylvan Safekeeper.
- **Replaying lands from the graveyard:** Ramunap Excavator, Szarel, Genesis Shepherd, Conduit of Worlds and Walk-In Closet.
- **Landfall payoffs:** Omnath, Locus of Rage, Rampaging Baloths, Scute Swarm, Tannuk, Memorial Ensign and Lotus Cobra.
- **Sacrifice payoffs:** Hearthhull, Korvold, Fae-Cursed King, Mayhem Devil, Titania, Protector of Argoth, Baloth Prime and Scouring Swarm.

A replayed fetchland fires both landfall and sacrifice triggers.

## Opening hands
Keep hands with three or more lands and either a ramp spell or a green two- or three-drop. Land-heavy hands are fine, because this deck spends lands as a resource. Mulligan hands that can't make green early, since most of the engine is green. Fetches and the Overlook-style lands also count as colour fixing.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp first. Hearthhull does nothing alone. To station it, you tap another untapped creature, and only at sorcery speed, so have one in play before you cast it. Station adds counters equal to that creature's power: Horizon Explorer adds 2, each Baloth Prime Beast token adds 4, and Baloth Prime itself adds 10.

At 2 or more counters, Hearthhull draws: pay {1}, tap it and sacrifice a land to draw two cards and gain an extra land drop. At 8 counters it becomes a creature with flying, vigilance and haste, and every land you sacrifice drains each opponent for 2. Reaching 8 is the turning point, so get there fast.

Baloth Prime enters tapped with six stun counters. Sacrifice a land the turn you cast it to start the 4/4 Beasts coming. Each sacrifice also removes a stun counter. The Beasts enter tapped, so they station next turn.

## How it wins
- **Drain with Hearthhull at 8.** Sacrifice lands in bulk with Scapeshift, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth and replayed fetches. Mayhem Devil and Korvold add a ping or a card per sacrifice. The Gitrog Monster draws as lands hit your graveyard.
- **Count for lethal (seen once).** Zuran Orb and Sylvan Safekeeper are free instant-speed outlets. Before tapping lands for spells, compare twice your land count with each opponent's life.
- **Build a board.** Scapeshift or Splendid Reclamation with Omnath or Rampaging Baloths makes a board of tokens in one turn. Moraug, Fury of Akoum turns each main-phase land drop into an extra combat.
- **Finish** with Worldsoul's Rage or Valakut Exploration.
- **Planetary Annihilation** deals 6 to each creature. Hearthhull at 8 and Baloth Prime survive it, and its land sacrifices fire all your triggers.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- Answer graveyard hate and anything that stops sacrifices or landfall.
- Answer flyers and fast combo with Infernal Grasp, Putrefy, Beast Within and Tear Asunder; kicked Tear Asunder exiles.
- Rakdos Charm is your cheap instant artifact answer. Pest Infestation handles several artifacts or enchantments.
- Save Putrefy for a creature threatening commander-damage lethal, cast in your main phase while its controller is tapped out (seen once).
- Blasphemous Act kills your engine too. Cast it only when behind.
- Race slow, fair creature decks with drain rather than spending removal.
- Sylvan Safekeeper protects a key creature from targeted removal.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Casting Hearthhull with no untapped creature to station it.** Build toward 8 counters in a single station.
- **Holding Hearthhull back for fear of removal (seen once).** Two untapped Beasts return it straight to 8 with haste; removal costs only the commander tax.
- **Spending Rakdos Charm's graveyard exile on a single target (seen once).** Save it, or Bojuka Bog, for effects that return a whole graveyard.
- **Destroying a creature its controller can make indestructible at instant speed, including through available mana (seen once).** Use kicked Tear Asunder, or wait until they can't pay.
