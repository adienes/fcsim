# Hearthhull, the Worldseed

## Gameplan
Lands are your resource, not just your mana. Play extra lands, sacrifice them, replay them from the graveyard, and convert each step into damage, cards and bodies.

- **Extra land drops:** Azusa, Lost but Seeking, Exploration, Oracle of Mul Daya, Icetill Explorer, The Gitrog Monster, Exploration Broodship.
- **Land sacrifice:** fetches, Crop Rotation, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth, Scapeshift, Zuran Orb, Sylvan Safekeeper.
- **Lands back:** Ramunap Excavator, Szarel, Genesis Shepherd, Conduit of Worlds, Walk-In Closet, Icetill Explorer, Splendid Reclamation, Aftermath Analyst.
- **Landfall:** Omnath, Locus of Rage, Rampaging Baloths, Scute Swarm, Tannuk, Memorial Ensign, Lotus Cobra, Moraug, Fury of Akoum.
- **Sacrifice payoffs:** Hearthhull, Korvold, Fae-Cursed King, Mayhem Devil, Titania, Protector of Argoth, Baloth Prime, Scouring Swarm.

A replayed fetchland fires both halves at once.

## Opening hands
Three or four lands beside a green one- to three-mana accelerant — Nature's Lore, Farseek, Cultivate, Lotus Cobra, Springbloom Druid, Exploration, Azusa — or a green three-drop. With 41 lands you are never short of drops, so lands without action are not a keep: five lands, Sol Ring and Pest Infestation is a mulligan, because Sol Ring's mana does nothing for an {X}{X}{G} sorcery. Mulligan hands that cannot make green early. Fetches and the Overlook-style lands count as fixing.

## Early turns and the commander
Ramp first. Hearthhull does nothing alone, and Station is sorcery-speed only — have a creature in play the turn *before* you cast it. Counters equal the creature's power: Horizon Explorer adds 2, a Baloth Prime Beast adds 4, Baloth Prime itself adds 10.

Station at every sorcery-speed opportunity with whatever you have. Counters persist and accumulate across turns; a 2/4 held back as a speculative blocker usually blocks nothing. Do not skip a 4-counter station to keep two small bodies untapped.

At 2, pay {1}, tap, sacrifice a land: draw two and take an extra land drop. At 8 it gains flying, vigilance and haste. Get there fast.

The drain clause has no station-level prefix: **from the moment Hearthhull is on the battlefield, at 0 counters as at 8, every land you sacrifice drains each opponent for 2.** A land sacrificed to pay a cost counts — Hearthhull's own ability, a Wooded Foothills activation, Cabaretti Courtyard, Roiling Regrowth's additional cost — and Mayhem Devil, Korvold, Mazirek and Titania all fire with it.

Cast Baloth Prime and sacrifice a land the same turn: each sacrifice makes a Beast and removes a stun counter. The Beasts enter tapped, so they station next turn.

When Hearthhull dies, move it to the command zone the moment it hits the graveyard. Removal costs you only the tax: recast, then tap two 4-power Beasts to station it straight back to 8 with haste, and the drain and draw work that same turn.

## How it wins
- **Drain.** Sacrifice lands in bulk with Scapeshift, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth and replayed fetches. Mayhem Devil pings, Korvold draws, Gitrog draws.
- **Count for lethal (seen once).** Zuran Orb and Sylvan Safekeeper are free, instant-speed outlets. Before you tap lands for spells, compare lands × 2 against each opponent's life. The whole mana base is a kill.
- **Board.** Scapeshift or Splendid Reclamation with Omnath or Rampaging Baloths builds an army in one turn. Moraug turns each main-phase land drop into an extra combat.
- **Finish** with Valakut Exploration, or Worldsoul's Rage held until X kills a player — its X also rebuilds your lands (seen once).
- **Planetary Annihilation:** Hearthhull at 8 and Baloth Prime survive the 6 damage, and the land sacrifices feed you.

## What to answer, what to ignore
- Answer graveyard hate and anything that stops sacrifices or landfall.
- Answer flyers and fast combo: Infernal Grasp, Putrefy, Beast Within, kicked Tear Asunder (exiles).
- Rakdos Charm is your cheap instant artifact answer *and* your graveyard exile. Save the exile mode for effects returning a whole graveyard, such as mass land returns — never for one flashback or one reanimation target. Bojuka Bog likewise.
- Blasphemous Act kills your engine; cast it only when behind.
- Race slow fair creature decks with drain instead of spending removal.
- Sylvan Safekeeper protects a key creature from targeted removal.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Casting Hearthhull with no untapped creature to station it.**
- **Destroy removal into instant-speed indestructibility (seen once).** Fire Putrefy on your main phase while the controller is tapped out, or use kicked Tear Asunder. Holding Putrefy for rounds is fine — spend it once a creature threatens a commander-damage kill.
- **Counting deaths as sacrifices (seen once).** Mazirek, Evendo Brushrazer and Hearthhull's drain ignore creatures killed by wraths, removal or damage.
- **Leaving an opponent alive on purpose.** Finish players when the drain reaches them; a pending trigger is not a kill.
