# Obeka, Splitter of Seconds — Primer

## Gameplan
Build a board of "at the beginning of your upkeep" permanents, then make Obeka connect. Each point of combat damage she deals is another upkeep after combat. Each extra upkeep fires every trigger again: Courts, Phyrexian Arena, Indulgent Tormentor, Twilight Prophet, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Plargg and Nassari, and suspend counters. Paradox Haze on yourself adds an upkeep every turn, even without combat. The draw step never repeats, so your cards come from upkeep triggers.

## Opening hands
The deck has 36 lands and ten mana rocks, and Obeka costs four. Keep these hands:
- 3–4 lands, or 2 lands plus Sol Ring or a Signet/Talisman.
- One cheap engine piece: Phyrexian Arena, Creeping Bloodsucker, Generous Plunderer, a suspend card, or Paradox Haze.

Mulligan seven-card hands that are all expensive Courts with no acceleration. Many duals enter tapped, so play them on turns when you have nothing to cast.

## Early turns and the commander
- **Turns 1–3:** Cast rocks. Suspend Ancestral Vision, Profane Tutor, Inspiring Refrain and Suspended Sentence early, because Obeka's upkeeps fast-forward them. Nightscape Familiar discounts most of your spells.
- **Courts:** Land one Court before Obeka if you can. Being monarch keeps the Courts at full strength and draws a card. Skyline Despot also makes you monarch.
- **Casting Obeka:** Cast her when she can hit that turn or survive the table.
  - Haste: Swiftfoot Boots, Lightning Greaves, Ring of Valkas.
  - Protection: Swiftfoot Boots, Whispersilk Cloak, Winged Boots, Brotherhood Regalia (equip {1} on her), Ring of Evos Isle.
  - Unblockable: Aether Tunnel, Aqueous Form, Whispersilk Cloak, Brotherhood Regalia. Rogue's Passage and Thassa's {1}{U} work situationally.
- **Growing her:** Rings of Valkas, Xathrid and Evos Isle each add a +1/+1 counter every upkeep, including extra ones. With Lizard Blades, her first-strike damage creates upkeeps that grow her before regular damage.

## How it wins
- **Damage and life loss:**
  - Court of Ire deals 7 per upkeep while you are monarch.
  - Court of Ambition makes each opponent lose 6 or discard two.
  - Court of Embereth makes Knights and scaling burn.
  - Palace Siege (Dragons), Creeping Bloodsucker and Scrawling Crawler drain.
- **Milling:** Court of Cunning mills ten per upkeep while you are monarch.
- **Mechanized Production:** Put it on a Treasure or rock. It wins at eight copies, which takes seven upkeeps.
- **Copying:** Extravagant Replication copies your best Court. Court of Vantress copies an artifact or enchantment. Replicating Ring and Rousing Refrain make mana.
- **Speed:** Two or three good triggers plus one Obeka hit for 4+ usually ends the game that turn.

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer:**
- Anything that removes or taps Obeka, especially fliers and first-strikers that block her.
- Enchantment removal aimed at Courts.
- Creatures that can steal the monarch.
- Fogs.

**Your tools:**
- Removal: Bedevil, Terminate, Feed the Swarm, Suspended Sentence.
- Chaos Warp handles anything.
- Counters: Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Negate, An Offer You Can't Refuse. Save them for wraths and removal aimed at your engine.

**Ignore:** Slow value engines and ground creatures that can't block a menace or unblockable Obeka.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Rushing Obeka:** Don't commit her before at least one upkeep payoff is on the battlefield. A hit with nothing to trigger is wasted.
- **Too few triggers:** Paradox Haze plus a Court beats Obeka alone.
- **Unprotected Obeka (seen once):** Cast onto an empty board, she died to removal. Land Silent Arbiter first. It allows only one blocker, and her menace needs two, so she can't be blocked. Its one-attacker limit costs you nothing. Then cast her with protection or haste.
- **Early Blasphemous Act (seen once):** Hold it for a crowded board. There it costs only {R}, which leaves mana for a Court the same turn. It kills Obeka too, so recast her afterward.
