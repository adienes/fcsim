# Obeka, Splitter of Seconds — Primer

## Gameplan
Build a board of "at the beginning of your upkeep" permanents, then make Obeka connect. Each point of combat damage she deals to a player gives you one extra upkeep right after combat. Every upkeep fires all your triggers again:
- Courts, Phyrexian Arena, Indulgent Tormentor, Twilight Prophet.
- Bonehoard Dracosaur, Plargg and Nassari.
- Suspend counters.

Paradox Haze on yourself adds an upkeep every turn. The draw step never repeats, so your cards come from upkeep triggers.

## Opening hands
The deck has 36 lands and ten mana rocks, and Obeka costs four. Keep a hand with both of these:
- 3–4 lands, or 2 lands plus Sol Ring, a Signet or a Talisman.
- One cheap engine piece: Phyrexian Arena, Creeping Bloodsucker, Generous Plunderer, a suspend card or Paradox Haze.

Mulligan hands that hold only expensive Courts and no acceleration. Many duals enter tapped, so play them on turns when you have nothing to cast.

## Early turns and the commander
- **Turns 1–3:** Cast rocks. Nightscape Familiar discounts your blue and red spells. On turn 2 or 3, suspend Ancestral Vision, Profane Tutor, Inspiring Refrain or Suspended Sentence. Obeka's extra upkeeps tick them down.
- **Courts:** Land a Court before Obeka if you can. Being monarch keeps Courts at full strength and draws a card each end step. Skyline Despot also makes you monarch.
- **Casting Obeka:** Cast her when she can hit that turn or survive the table.
  - Haste: Swiftfoot Boots, Lightning Greaves, Ring of Valkas.
  - Protection: Swiftfoot Boots, Whispersilk Cloak, Winged Boots, Brotherhood Regalia, Ring of Evos Isle.
  - Evasion: Aether Tunnel, Aqueous Form, Whispersilk Cloak, Brotherhood Regalia, Rogue's Passage, Thassa, God of the Sea. Silent Arbiter plus her menace makes her unblockable (seen once).
- **Growing her:** Ring of Valkas, Ring of Xathrid and Ring of Evos Isle each add a +1/+1 counter every upkeep, extra ones included. Lizard Blades' double strike doubles the upkeeps she earns.

## How it wins
- **Drain:** Court of Ire deals 7 per upkeep while you are monarch. Court of Ambition makes each opponent lose 6 life or discard two cards. Court of Embereth, Palace Siege (Dragons), Creeping Bloodsucker, Scrawling Crawler and Twilight Prophet also drain.
- **Mill:** Court of Cunning mills ten per upkeep while you are monarch.
- **Mechanized Production:** On a rock, it wins after seven upkeeps. On Replicating Ring, once eight Replicated Rings exist, the next trigger wins.
- **Copying:** Extravagant Replication copies your best Court. Court of Vantress copies an artifact or enchantment.
- **Speed:** Two or three good triggers plus one Obeka hit for 4 or more usually ends the game that turn.

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer:**
- Anything that removes or taps Obeka, especially fliers and first-strikers that can block her.
- Enchantment removal aimed at Courts.
- Creatures that can take the monarch.
- Fogs.

**Your tools:** Bedevil, Terminate, Feed the Swarm, Suspended Sentence and Chaos Warp. Save Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Negate and An Offer You Can't Refuse for wraths and for removal aimed at your engine.

**Ignore:** Slow value engines, and ground creatures that can't block a menace or unblockable Obeka.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Rushing Obeka:** Don't commit her before at least one upkeep payoff is on the battlefield. A hit with nothing to trigger is wasted.
- **Too few triggers:** Paradox Haze plus a Court beats Obeka alone.
- **Seen once:**
  - Cast Obeka with haste or protection, not onto an empty board.
  - Cast Courts and suspend cards in main phase 1 on the turn she connects.
  - Attack the opponent with the fewest untapped blockers, not the lowest life total.
  - Hold Blasphemous Act for a crowded board. It kills Obeka too.
