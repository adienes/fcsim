# Obeka, Splitter of Seconds — Primer

## Gameplan
Build a board of "at the beginning of your upkeep" permanents, then make Obeka connect. Each point of combat damage she deals a player gives you one extra upkeep right after that phase, and every upkeep fires all your triggers again: Courts, Phyrexian Arena, Indulgent Tormentor, Twilight Prophet, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Plargg and Nassari, and suspend counters. Paradox Haze on yourself adds an upkeep every turn, hit or no hit. The draw step never repeats; your cards come from upkeep triggers.

## Opening hands
36 lands and ten rocks, and Obeka costs four. Keep hands with both:
- 3–4 lands, or 2 lands plus Sol Ring, a Signet or a Talisman.
- One cheap engine piece: Phyrexian Arena, Creeping Bloodsucker, Generous Plunderer, Paradox Haze, or a suspend card.

Mulligan hands of expensive Courts with no acceleration. Play tapped duals on turns you have nothing to cast.

## Early turns and the commander
The clean curve: suspend Inspiring Refrain, Ancestral Vision, Profane Tutor or Suspended Sentence on turn 2 or 3, cast Obeka on turn 4, attack on turn 5. Every extra upkeep removes another time counter, so the card already sitting in exile is her first payoff — nothing else need be on board yet.

Suspend ticks in *every* upkeep you take, the normal one included. Put the tick on the stack beside Phyrexian Arena each time. Suspended Sentence is repeating removal, not a one-shot: it re-exiles itself with three fresh counters on resolution, so pick its next target before you attack.

**Silent Arbiter is the key enabler.** Menace needs two blockers; Arbiter permits only one. Land Arbiter first, then cast Obeka on a turn you can protect her or give her haste (Swiftfoot Boots, Lightning Greaves, Ring of Valkas) so she connects before the table answers. Arbiter also means she is your only attacker — fine, she is the only one who matters.

Other evasion: Aether Tunnel, Aqueous Form, Whispersilk Cloak, Brotherhood Regalia, Winged Boots, Rogue's Passage, Thassa. The three Rings add a +1/+1 counter every upkeep, extra ones included, so she grows mid-combat-cycle. Lizard Blades doubles the upkeeps a hit earns.

## How it wins
- **Drain:** Court of Ire deals 7 per upkeep as monarch. Court of Ambition drains 6 unless they discard two. Court of Embereth, Palace Siege (Dragons), Creeping Bloodsucker, Scrawling Crawler and Twilight Prophet stack up. Note Twilight Prophet: each opponent loses X, but you gain X *once*, not per opponent.
- **Mill:** Court of Cunning, ten per upkeep as monarch.
- **Mechanized Production:** on any rock it wins on the seventh trigger. After Replicating Ring makes its eight tokens, enchant one and the next trigger wins.
- **Copying:** Extravagant Replication on your best Court; Court of Vantress on any artifact or enchantment.
- Two or three live triggers plus one 4-damage hit usually ends it that turn.

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer:** anything that removes or taps Obeka; fliers and first-strikers that can block her; enchantment removal aimed at Courts; fogs; and the creature coming to take your crown — hold Terminate for it and keep a body back. Casting a Court onto an empty board hands the monarch to the next attacker.

**Tools:** Bedevil, Terminate, Feed the Swarm, Suspended Sentence, Chaos Warp. Save Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Negate and An Offer You Can't Refuse for wraths and for removal aimed at your engine. Blasphemous Act costs {1} less per creature — hold it for a crowded board, where it costs {R} and leaves mana for a Court the same turn. It kills Obeka too.

**Ignore:** slow value engines, and ground creatures that can't block a menacing Obeka.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Rushing Obeka.** A hit with nothing to trigger is wasted.
- **Too few triggers.** Paradox Haze plus a Court beats Obeka alone.
- **Attacking by life total.** Send her at whoever has one untapped creature or none; a single blocker can't stop menace. Two damage is two whole upkeeps.
- **Seen once:** sequence everything into main phase 1 on the turn she connects — Courts cast, cards suspended. The extra upkeeps come right after combat, and main phase 2 plays miss them entirely.
- **Seen once:** shroud and hexproof don't stop edicts. Against sacrifice effects, keep two spare bodies — Embereth Knights, Nightscape Familiar, Dragonmaster Outcast — before leaning on Obeka staying put.
