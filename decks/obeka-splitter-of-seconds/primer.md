# Obeka, Splitter of Seconds — Primer

## Gameplan
Build a board of "at the beginning of your upkeep" permanents, then make Obeka connect. Each point of combat damage she deals to a player gives you one extra upkeep right after combat. Every upkeep fires all your triggers again: Courts, Phyrexian Arena, Indulgent Tormentor, Twilight Prophet, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Plargg and Nassari, and suspend counters. Paradox Haze on yourself adds an upkeep every turn. The draw step never repeats, so your cards come from upkeep triggers.

## Opening hands
The deck has 36 lands and ten mana rocks, and Obeka costs four. Keep hands with both:
- 3–4 lands, or 2 lands plus Sol Ring, a Signet or a Talisman.
- One cheap engine piece: Phyrexian Arena, Creeping Bloodsucker, Generous Plunderer, a suspend card or Paradox Haze.

Mulligan hands that hold only expensive Courts and no acceleration. Play tapped duals on turns when you have nothing to cast.

## Early turns and the commander
- **Turns 1–3:** Cast rocks. Nightscape Familiar discounts blue and red spells. Suspend Ancestral Vision, Profane Tutor, Inspiring Refrain or Suspended Sentence early. Obeka's extra upkeeps tick them down fast.
- **Silent Arbiter:** Only one creature can block, so menace makes Obeka unblockable. Only one creature can attack each combat, so she is your only attacker.
- **Casting Obeka:** Cast her only when she can hit this turn or survive the table.
  - Haste: Swiftfoot Boots, Lightning Greaves, Ring of Valkas.
  - Protection: Swiftfoot Boots, Whispersilk Cloak, Winged Boots, Brotherhood Regalia, Ring of Evos Isle.
  - Evasion: Aether Tunnel, Aqueous Form, Whispersilk Cloak, Brotherhood Regalia, Rogue's Passage, Thassa, God of the Sea.
- **Growing her:** Ring of Valkas, Ring of Xathrid and Ring of Evos Isle add a +1/+1 counter every upkeep, extra ones included. Lizard Blades' double strike doubles the upkeeps she earns.

## How it wins
- **Drain:** Court of Ire deals 7 to any target per upkeep while you are monarch. Court of Ambition makes each opponent lose 6 life unless they discard two cards. Court of Embereth, Palace Siege (Dragons), Creeping Bloodsucker, Scrawling Crawler and Twilight Prophet also drain.
- **Mill:** Court of Cunning mills ten per upkeep while you are monarch.
- **Mechanized Production:** On any rock, it wins on the seventh trigger. After Replicating Ring makes its eight Replicated Rings, put it on one of them and the next trigger wins.
- **Copying:** Extravagant Replication copies your best Court. Court of Vantress copies an artifact or enchantment.
- **Speed:** Two or three good triggers plus one Obeka hit for 4 or more usually ends the game that turn.

## What to answer, what to ignore
**Answer:**
- Anything that removes or taps Obeka, especially fliers and first-strikers that can block her.
- Enchantment removal aimed at Courts.
- Creatures that can take the monarch. Don't cast a Court onto an empty board. Save Terminate for the creature going for the crown and keep a blocker back (seen once).
- Fogs.

**Your tools:** Bedevil, Terminate, Feed the Swarm, Suspended Sentence and Chaos Warp remove threats. Save Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Negate and An Offer You Can't Refuse for wraths and for removal aimed at your engine.

**Ignore:** Slow value engines, and ground creatures that can't block a menace or unblockable Obeka.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
- **Rushing Obeka:** Don't commit her before at least one upkeep payoff or suspended card is in place. A hit with nothing to trigger is wasted.
- **Too few triggers:** Paradox Haze plus a Court beats Obeka alone.
- **Seen once:**
  - On the turn she connects, cast Courts and suspend cards in main phase 1. Main phase 2 plays miss the extra upkeeps.
  - Attack the opponent with the fewest untapped creatures, not the lowest life total. One lone creature can't block her menace.
  - Shroud and hexproof don't stop edicts. Keep two spare creatures, such as Court of Embereth Knights, Nightscape Familiar or Dragonmaster Outcast.
  - Hold Blasphemous Act for a crowded board, where it costs as little as {R}. It kills Obeka too.
