# Lessons from 2026-09-21_201717_pod-wilhelt-obeka-hearthhull-fire-lord-z_0051

- **how_a_card_works** Suspend removes a time counter in every one of your upkeeps, the normal one included, as well as each extra upkeep Obeka earns. Put the tick on the stack every upkeep beside Phyrexian Arena's trigger. A card suspended with 2 counters on your turn N is cast free in your upkeep two turns later. _Evidence: Game 0051: Profane Tutor was suspended in round 4 (table turn 16). The pilot missed its tick in the round 5 upkeep (turn 20) and again in the normal upkeep of round 6 (turn 24), so it went off a whole step late, in Obeka's first extra upkeep. The auditor flagged both misses._
- **sequencing** When a suspended Profane Tutor goes off during Obeka's extra upkeeps, take an upkeep payoff that multiplies the next hit, such as Paradox Haze, Court of Ambition or Court of Ire. Don't take a mana rock once you already have 5 lands. _Evidence: Game 0051, round 6 (turn 24): Obeka's 2-damage hit ticked Profane Tutor to 0. It fetched Sol Ring with 5 lands in play and Court of Embereth already castable next turn. By the time Obeka was cast again in round 8 she was killed, and the tutor had added no trigger to the board._
- **threat_assessment** Lightning Greaves' shroud and Swiftfoot Boots' hexproof do not stop edicts. Against Fleshbag Marauder or Grave Pact, have at least two spare creatures (Court of Embereth Knights, Nightscape Familiar, Dragonmaster Outcast) before you rely on Obeka staying in play. Expect one sacrifice for each creature the Grave Pact player loses, including Wilhelt's end-step sacrifice. _Evidence: Game 0051: Wilhelt showed Fleshbag Marauder in round 4 (turn 15) and cast Grave Pact in round 7 (turn 27). That turn the decayed Zombie's end-of-combat death took Dragonmaster Outcast. Then Wilhelt's end-step sacrifice of Gravecrawler took Obeka, even though she was wearing Greaves._
- **commander_timing** Game 0051, round 8 (turn 32): the pilot recast Obeka for 6 with tax while Wilhelt had Dimir Aqueduct untapped. Go for the Throat killed her before Greaves could be equipped, and the pilot was left at 5 life with most of its mana spent. _Evidence: Game 0051, round 8 (turn 32): the pilot recast Obeka for 6 with tax while Wilhelt had Dimir Aqueduct untapped. - **interaction** A Court makes you monarch, and staying monarch is what keeps it at full strength. Hold removal like Terminate for the creature attacking to take the crown, and keep a body back to block. Casting a Court onto an empty board just hands the monarch to the next attacker. _Evidence: Game 0051: Court of Embereth came down in round 7 (turn 28). On turn 30, Terminate on the attacking Emberheart Challenger kept the monarch. On turn 31 Obeka had no creatures, so Wilhelt's attack took the monarch. The Court's upkeep trigger on turn 32 then made a Knight but dealt no damage. The pilot won the crown back that turn by attacking with the Greaves-equipped Knight._
