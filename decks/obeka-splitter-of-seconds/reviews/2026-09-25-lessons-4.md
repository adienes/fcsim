- **how_a_card_works** Suspended Sentence is repeating removal, not a one-shot: its own text re-exiles it with three fresh time counters every time it resolves. Suspend it once for {1}{B} and every upkeep you take — your normal one plus each extra upkeep Obeka earns — ticks it down again, so decide the next target before you attack rather than after. _Evidence: Game 0061: suspended on turn 11 for {1}{B}; turn 15 Obeka's 2-damage hit gave two extra upkeeps that stripped its last counters and it destroyed Rampaging Baloths for free, then went back to exile with 3 counters; turn 19 two more extra upkeeps emptied it again and it destroyed Fire Lord Zuko (sending him to the command zone) and re-exiled with 3 counters once more. Two free removal spells from one 2-mana investment in four turns._
