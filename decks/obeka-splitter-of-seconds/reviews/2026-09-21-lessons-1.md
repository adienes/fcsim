# Lessons from 2026-09-21_071131_pod-fire-lord-zuko-gishath-avatar-aang-a_0011, 2026-09-21_105026_pod-obeka-fire-lord-zuko-ms-bumbleflower_0023

- **sequencing** Silent Arbiter plus Obeka's menace means Obeka can't be blocked: menace needs two blockers, and Arbiter allows only one blocker per combat. Land Arbiter first. Then cast Obeka on a turn when you can protect her from targeted removal and fights, or give her haste (Swiftfoot Boots, Lightning Greaves, Ring of Valkas) so she connects before the table answers her. _Evidence: In game _0011 it cast Silent Arbiter in round 4 and Obeka in round 5, correctly noting that she was unblockable. It tapped out for Dragonmaster Outcast instead of protecting her, and on Gishath's next turn Apex Altisaur fought and killed Obeka and Arbiter before she ever attacked._
- **interaction** Hold Blasphemous Act until the board is crowded. It costs {1} less for each creature on the battlefield, so on a big board it costs only {R} and still leaves mana for a follow-up play like a Court the same turn. _Evidence: In game _0011, round 7 (table turn 28), 16 creatures were on the battlefield. The pilot cast Blasphemous Act for {R}, killing 13 creatures including Ghalta, Apex Altisaur and Etali, then cast Court of Cunning with the lands left over._

## How it was piloted, game by game

- `2026-09-21_071131_pod-fire-lord-zuko-gishath-avatar-aang-a_0011` **Keep:** Kept 7 with no mulligan: 5 lands (Xander's Lounge, Morphic Pool, Sunken Hollow, Drowned Catacomb, Exotic Orchard), no mana rocks, and only Dragonmaster Outcast and Negate as spells. Outcast does nothing before six lands and Negate only reacts, so the hand had no play for turns 2 and 3. It cast nothing in rounds 1–3; its first spell was Silent Arbiter in round 4. The first mulligan is free, and this hand should have gone back for one with a two-mana rock or a real two- or three-drop. **Mana:** No land drops missed. In rounds 2 and 3 it ended with Dragonmaster Outcast castable, holding up Negate. Holding the Outcast was defensible: it makes nothing below six lands and is easy to kill. In round 3, though, it played Sunken Hollow tapped when Drowned Catacomb would have entered untapped (Xander's Lounge is an Island Swamp). That was a small waste. In round 6 it held Court of Cunning with Negate up, reasoning that becoming monarch in front of Gishath's board would draw attacks. That was reasonable, and the Negate later saved it from Akroma's Will. **Attacks:** The one turn flagged with an attacker kept home was round 5. Obeka had just been cast and was summoning sick, so the only possible attacker was Silent Arbiter, a 1/5 better used as a blocker. That was not a mistake.
