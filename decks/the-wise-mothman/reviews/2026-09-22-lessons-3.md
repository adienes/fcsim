- **sequencing** Tato Farmer untaps only on your own turn. If you tap it on an opponent's turn to take a milled land, it cannot block until your next untap. Take the land only when you don't need Tato as a blocker before your next turn; a 6/9 Tato is worth more than an extra land when you are at 17. _Evidence: Game 0052, turn 28 (Tidus's turn): it tapped Tato Farmer to take Overflowing Basin. On turn 29 Zuko attacked and it declared no blocks because all three of its creatures were tapped, then died to 25 damage._
- **sequencing** Cast Nuclear Fallout with X=2 before Mothman, not after. The -4/-4 kills a freshly cast 3/3 Mothman along with the opposing early threats. Cast Mothman into the empty board the next turn, and remember the 2 rad counters the Fallout gives each player are milled at each player's next precombat main phase. Mothman gets no counters from those mills unless he is already in play. _Evidence: Game 0052, round 4: with six mana, enough for Mothman, it cast Fallout X=2 instead. That killed Terror of the Peaks, Kami of Whispered Hopes, Electro and Headliner Scarlett, and it cast Mothman in round 5. The rad mills on turns 16 to 19 turned up nonland cards (Tidus: Gyre Sage, Kutzil; Zuko: Appa; Miirym: Astral Dragon, Atarka; its own: Ruin Crab, Rampant Growth), but Mothman was not yet in play, so they gave no counters._
- **how_a_card_works** Resolve Mothman's trigger on every opponent's radiation mill during their turn. Spread the counters over every creature you have, because the counters are capped by the number of targets. Once they have rad counters, a Mothman grown this way is a flying answer to planeswalkers. (Game 0052: opponents' rad mills that hit nonland cards put six of his counters on him, and his own rad mills and Maddening Cacophony put on the other three, taking him from 3/3 to 12/12.) _Evidence: Game 0052, turns 20 to 29: rad mills on Tidus's, Zuko's and Miirym's turns put a counter on Mothman each time and took him from 3/3 to 12/12.
