# Lessons from 2026-09-20_230513_pod-fire-lord-zuko-ashling-aragorn-the-w_0008, 2026-09-21_123655_pod-the-wise-mothman-edgar-markov-tidus-_0031

- **how_a_card_works** Every time any player mills a nonland card, The Wise Mothman triggers: an opponent's radiation mill at the start of their precombat main, Ruin Crab, Mindcrank, The Water Crystal's extra cards. So does Glowing One, once for each nonland card. Put these triggers on the stack as soon as the mill resolves, on the opponent's turn, and target Hancock or Mothman. Don't wait for your own upkeep. _Evidence: Game 0008: the auditor logged Mothman or Glowing One triggers never put on the stack on turns 7, 8, 17, 19, 20, 22 and 23. Turn 19 alone was 9 nonland cards: 9 life and counters on three creatures, only recovered late. Game 0031: the Mothman trigger was missed on turns 12, 14, 16 and 17 after each opponent's rad mill. Each had to be caught up in the next upkeep, and one trigger (X=2) came from a mill that happened just before Valefor bounced Mothman._
- **sequencing** Once Ruin Crab is in play, play your land drop and your land ramp (Nature's Lore, Rampant Growth, Farseek, Cultivate, Evolving Wilds) on the same turn Mothman is out. Each landfall mills every opponent 3, and the nonland cards among those turn into that many +1/+1 counters spread over Mothman, Hancock and the Crab. _Evidence: Game 0031, round 4 (turn 15): with Mothman and Hancock already out, the pilot played Tainted Wood and then cast Nature's Lore for Breeding Pool. The two Crab mills hit 6 and 7 nonland cards. Mothman became a 10/10 flier and hit Edgar for 10 commander damage that same turn._
- **interaction** When the table has named Mothman as the threat, keep 1GU open for Mutational Advantage and skip a creature to do it. It gives hexproof and indestructible to everything with counters, fizzles targeted exile, and its proliferate grows Mothman and every player's rad counters too. _Evidence: Game 0008, round 4: the pilot kept Hancock in hand so Mutational Advantage stayed castable. In round 5, Aragorn aimed Éowyn, Fearless Knight's exile at Mothman. Mutational Advantage countered it by making the target illegal, and Mothman (then on 15 commander damage) killed Aragorn the next turn._
- **threat_assessment** Cast The Water Crystal as soon as opponents are carrying rad counters. It adds four cards to every opponent's radiation mill, so each rad counter turns into more life loss for them, more Mothman counters and more Glowing One life. Infesting Radroach and Glowing One hits then become a finisher. _Evidence: Game 0008, round 5: after the Crystal came down, Zuko's rad mill became 11 cards (9 nonland) and took him to 19. In round 7, Radroach gave Ashling 6 rad counters at 2 life, and her next mill of 7+4=11 cards killed her._
- **combat** Put Mothman's mill counters on Hancock, Ghoulish Mayor whenever you can: each counter on him gives +1/+1 to Mothman, Bloatfly Swarm, Glowing One and every other Zombie or Mutant. Once he has a counter, undying won't bring him back, so keep him out of combat and away from first strikers. _Evidence: Game 0031, turn 17: one counter on Hancock made Mothman 11/11. On turn 16, facing Edgar's first-strike vampires, the pilot held Hancock back so it wouldn't lose +2 on Mothman. Game 0008, round 6: Hancock with two counters stayed home while his anthem made the other attackers big enough to kill Zuko._
