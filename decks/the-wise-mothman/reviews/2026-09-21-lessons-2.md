# Lessons from 2026-09-21_173349_pod-the-wise-mothman-fire-lord-zuko-tidu_0041

- **interaction** Once Mothman has become the table's threat, keep open whatever protection you hold on each opponent's turn and during your own attack: Heroic Intervention (1G), as well as Mutational Advantage. Play only a cheap spell beside it so the protection stays available. _Evidence: Game 0041: from round 5 on, the pilot left Forest and Temple of Malady untapped for Heroic Intervention. In round 6 Tidus cast Path to Exile on the attacking 17/17 Mothman. Intervention fizzled it, and the hit killed Tidus._
- **sequencing** With Ruin Crab in play, hold a land for the turn after Mothman lands, so that landfall mill turns into counters. _Evidence: Game 0041: in round 4 the Forest was needed to cast Mothman, so the pilot kept Exotic Orchard back. In round 5 it played the Orchard with Mothman out, and the Crab's mill of Laelia put a counter on both Mothman and the Crab._
