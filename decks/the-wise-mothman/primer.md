# The Wise Mothman Primer

## Gameplan
This is a Sultai +1/+1 counters deck that runs on milling. Each time nonland cards are milled, The Wise Mothman puts a +1/+1 counter on each of up to X target creatures, where X is the number of nonland cards milled. It gives one counter per creature, not X counters on one creature, so you need a board to spread them across. These cards feed the mill:

* Ruin Crab
* Mindcrank
* Syr Konrad, the Grim
* Raul, Trouble Shooter
* Mesmeric Orb
* The Water Crystal
* Fraying Sanity
* Maddening Cacophony
* Opponents' own radiation mills, which Mothman, Nightkin Ambusher, Vault 12 and the rest set up

These cards multiply every counter: Hardened Scales, Winding Constrictor, Branching Evolution, Corpsejack Menace and Kami of Whispered Hopes. Proliferate (Atomize, Radstorm, Inexorable Tide, Evolution Sage, Agent Frank Horrigan) grows your creatures and the opponents' rad counters at the same time.

## Opening hands
* Keep hands with three lands that cover at least two colors, or two lands plus Sol Ring, a Talisman, Arcane Signet or a two-mana land ramp spell.
* Mothman costs {1}{B}{G}{U}, so fix toward all three colors before you add spells.
* Ruin Crab, Winding Constrictor, Hardened Scales and Cathedral Acolyte are strong early plays.
* Mull away hands of six-drops.

## Early turns and the commander
* **Ramp and fix, then cast Mothman on turn 3 or 4.** Nature's Lore, Farseek, Rampant Growth and Cultivate set this up.
* **Sequence land drops around Ruin Crab (seen once).** Once the Crab is out, hold your land drop and land ramp for the turn Mothman is on the battlefield. Each landfall then mills every opponent three, and the nonland cards become counters on Mothman, Hancock and the Crab. Evolution Sage adds a proliferate to every one of those landfalls.
* **Mothman triggers on every mill, including on opponents' turns (seen once).** That includes an opponent's radiation mill in their precombat main phase, plus Ruin Crab, Mindcrank and The Water Crystal's extra cards. Target your creatures as each mill resolves. Glowing One gains 1 life per nonland card milled.
* **Put Mothman's counters on Hancock, Ghoulish Mayor (seen once).** Every counter on him gives +1/+1 to each other Zombie or Mutant. That includes Mothman, Bloatfly Swarm, Glowing One, Nightkin Ambusher and the Vault 12 tokens.

## How it wins
* **Mindcrank plus Bloodchief Ascension.** Once the Ascension has three quest counters, this pair loops: each card an opponent puts into their graveyard makes them lose 2, and Mindcrank mills them 2 more. Proliferate adds quest counters to speed the Ascension up.
* **Syr Konrad plus Mindcrank.** Konrad deals damage for every milled creature card, and Mindcrank mills again for that damage, so this pair chains too.
* **The Water Crystal (seen once).** Cast it as soon as opponents carry rad counters. Every mill gets four more cards, which means more radiation life loss, more Mothman counters and more Glowing One life.
* **Evasive beats.** Nightkin Ambusher is unblockable against rad-counter holders. Infesting Radroach and Glowing One hits add rad counters. Pumped fliers finish games, and Marcus, Mutant Mayor draws off every counter-bearing hit.
* **Big bodies.** Screeching Scorchbeast tokens, Vault 12, and Agent Frank Horrigan's double proliferate close games out.

## What to answer, what to ignore
* **Your answers:**
  * Assassin's Trophy
  * Putrefy
  * Atomize
  * Casualties of War
  * Alpha Deathclaw
  * Rampaging Yao Guai
  * Counterspell
* **Spend them on:** board wipes you can't protect against, graveyard hate, first strikers that eat your counters, and fast combo.
* **Ignore** slow value engines. Your mill punishes long games.
* **Protecting Mothman (seen once).** When the table names Mothman as the threat, hold {1}{G}{U} for Mutational Advantage rather than casting a creature. It only protects permanents that have counters, so make sure Mothman has one. Its proliferate also grows everyone's rad counters.
* **Other protection.** Heroic Intervention covers wipes, and Ripples of Potential can phase your key pieces out.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
* **Losing Hancock for good (seen once).** Once Hancock has a +1/+1 counter, undying won't return him. Keep him out of combat and away from first strikers.
* **Irradiating yourself.** Mothman's enter and attack triggers, Contaminated Drink, Nuclear Fallout and Vault 12 all give you rad counters too. Winding Constrictor adds one extra rad counter each time you get them. Strong, the Brutish Thespian turns your radiation into life gain.
* **Killing your own board.** Nuclear Fallout and Finality are symmetrical. Count what survives on your side before you cast either.
* **Aiming the mill at yourself.** Raul, Mesmeric Orb, Syr Konrad and Maddening Cacophony are not all one-sided. Raul, Konrad and Mesmeric Orb mill you too, so watch your own library in long games.
