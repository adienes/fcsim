# The Wise Mothman Primer

## Gameplan
This is a Sultai +1/+1 counters deck driven by mill. Whenever nonland cards are milled, The Wise Mothman puts a counter on each of up to X creatures, where X is the number of nonland cards milled. It spreads counters rather than stacking them, so build a wide board.
* **Mill:** Ruin Crab, Mindcrank, Syr Konrad, the Grim, Raul, Trouble Shooter, Mesmeric Orb, Fraying Sanity, Maddening Cacophony, The Water Crystal, and opponents' own radiation mills.
* **Doublers:** Hardened Scales, Winding Constrictor, Branching Evolution, Corpsejack Menace, Kami of Whispered Hopes.
* **Proliferate:** Evolution Sage, Atomize, Radstorm, Inexorable Tide and Agent Frank Horrigan grow your creatures and opponents' rad counters together.

## Opening hands
* Keep three lands covering two colors, or two lands plus Sol Ring, a Talisman, Arcane Signet or a two-mana ramp spell.
* Mothman costs {1}{B}{G}{U}, so fix toward all three colors first.
* Good early plays are Ruin Crab, Winding Constrictor, Hardened Scales and Cathedral Acolyte.
* Mulligan hands full of six-drops.

## Early turns and the commander
* **Cast Mothman on turn 3 or 4.** Use Nature's Lore, Farseek, Rampant Growth and Cultivate.
* **Hold land drops for Ruin Crab plus Mothman.** Once the Crab is out, save your land drop and land ramp, including Evolving Wilds, for a turn when Mothman is on the battlefield.
  * Each landfall mills every opponent three.
  * Every nonland card milled becomes a counter spread over Mothman, Hancock and the Crab.
  * Evolution Sage adds a proliferate to each landfall.
* **Mothman triggers on every mill, on any turn.** That includes an opponent's radiation mill in their precombat main, Mindcrank, and The Water Crystal's extra cards.
  * Put the trigger on the stack as each mill resolves and target Hancock or Mothman.
  * Glowing One gains you 1 life for each nonland card milled.
* **Aim counters at Hancock, Ghoulish Mayor (seen once).** Each counter on him pumps Mothman and your other Zombies and Mutants.
* **Cast Nuclear Fallout before Mothman (seen once).** At X=2 it kills a fresh 3/3 Mothman.

## How it wins
* **Mindcrank plus Bloodchief Ascension.** Once the Ascension has three quest counters, each card an opponent puts into their graveyard costs them 2 life. Mindcrank then mills them two more cards, so the pair loops. Proliferate adds quest counters.
* **Syr Konrad plus Mindcrank.** Every milled creature card deals 1 damage to each opponent, and Mindcrank mills them again for that damage.
* **The Water Crystal (seen once).** Cast it once opponents carry rad counters; every mill grows by four.
* **Bodies:** Nightkin Ambusher can't be blocked by players with rad counters; Marcus, Mutant Mayor draws off connecting creatures with counters; Screeching Scorchbeast, Vault 12: The Necropolis and Horrigan close.

## What to answer, what to ignore
* **Answers:** Assassin's Trophy, Putrefy, Atomize, Casualties of War, Alpha Deathclaw, Rampaging Yao Guai, Counterspell. Spend them on wipes you can't protect against, graveyard hate, first strikers and fast combo. Ignore slow value engines; your mill punishes long games.
* **Protect Mothman with Mutational Advantage.** Once the table names Mothman as the threat, hold {1}{G}{U} instead of casting a creature.
  * It gives everything with a counter hexproof and indestructible, which fizzles targeted exile.
  * Its proliferate grows Mothman and every rad counter.
  * Make sure Mothman has a counter first.
* Heroic Intervention covers wipes; Ripples of Potential phases key pieces out.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
* **Losing Hancock for good (seen once).** With a +1/+1 counter, undying won't return him; keep him out of combat.
* **Attacking with the only blocker (seen once).** Mothman lacks vigilance; keep him home when an opponent shows lethal.
* **Irradiating yourself.** Mothman's enter and attack triggers, Contaminated Drink, Nuclear Fallout and Vault 12 all give you rad counters, and Winding Constrictor adds one more each time. Strong, the Brutish Thespian turns your radiation into life gain.
* **Killing your own board.** Nuclear Fallout and Finality hit every creature, so count what survives on your side first.
* **Milling yourself out.** Raul, Syr Konrad's activation and Mesmeric Orb mill you too. Watch your library in long games.
