# The Wise Mothman Primer

## Gameplan
Sultai +1/+1 counters driven by mill. Whenever nonland cards are milled — by anyone, on any turn — The Wise Mothman puts a counter on each of up to X creatures, where X is the number of nonland cards milled. It spreads counters rather than stacking them, so **board width caps your payoff**: with five creatures out, a six-card mill is worth five counters.
* **Mill:** Ruin Crab, Mindcrank, Syr Konrad, the Grim, Raul, Trouble Shooter, Mesmeric Orb, Fraying Sanity, Maddening Cacophony, The Water Crystal, and every opponent's own radiation mill.
* **Doublers:** Hardened Scales, Winding Constrictor, Branching Evolution, Corpsejack Menace, Kami of Whispered Hopes.
* **Proliferate:** Evolution Sage, Atomize, Radstorm, Inexorable Tide, Agent Frank Horrigan — these grow your creatures and opponents' rad counters at once.

## Opening hands
* Keep three lands that between them make B, G and U, or two such lands plus Sol Ring, a Talisman, Arcane Signet or Nature's Lore.
* **Exotic Orchard makes only colors an opponent's land can already make.** A hand whose only blue source is Exotic Orchard has no blue.
* Mulligan any hand that cannot produce {U} and has nothing to cast before turn three. Mulligan hands full of six-drops.
* Good early plays: Ruin Crab, Winding Constrictor, Hardened Scales, Cathedral Acolyte.

## Early turns and the commander
* **Cast Mothman on turn 3 or 4.** Nature's Lore, Farseek, Rampant Growth and Cultivate get you there; slipping to turn 5 costs you the game.
* **Hold land drops for Ruin Crab plus Mothman.** Once the Crab is out, save your land drop and your land ramp — including Evolving Wilds — for a turn when Mothman is on the battlefield. Each landfall mills every opponent three, and every nonland card among them is a counter. Evolution Sage adds a proliferate per land.
* **Mothman triggers on opponents' turns.** Their radiation mill at the start of their precombat main, Mindcrank, The Water Crystal's extra four. Put the trigger on the stack as the mill resolves — don't wait for your upkeep — and target Hancock or Mothman. Glowing One gains 1 life per nonland card milled.
* **Feed Hancock, Ghoulish Mayor (seen once).** Each counter on him pumps Mothman, Bloatfly Swarm, Glowing One and every Zombie or Mutant. Cast him early; he does nothing in hand.
* **Cast Nuclear Fallout before Mothman, not after (seen once).** At X=2 the -4/-4 kills a fresh 3/3 Mothman.
* **Spare mana buys bodies, not equipment (seen once).** Hancock or Mirelurk Queen beats Nuka-Nuke Launcher's {2} plus {3} equip: more targets for the spread, and another blocker.

## How it wins
* **Mindcrank plus Bloodchief Ascension.** At three quest counters each card into an opponent's graveyard costs 2 life, which Mindcrank turns into two more cards. Proliferate supplies the quest counters.
* **Syr Konrad plus Mindcrank.** Each milled creature card pings every opponent, and the damage mills again.
* **The Water Crystal (seen once).** Cast it once opponents carry rad counters; every mill grows by four, feeding Mothman, Glowing One and Infesting Radroach.
* **Bodies:** Nightkin Ambusher is unblockable against anyone with a rad counter; Marcus, Mutant Mayor draws off connecting counter-creatures; Screeching Scorchbeast, Vault 12: The Necropolis and Horrigan close.

## What to answer, what to ignore
* **Answers:** Assassin's Trophy, Putrefy, Atomize, Casualties of War, Alpha Deathclaw, Rampaging Yao Guai, Counterspell. Spend them on graveyard hate, wipes you can't protect against, first strikers and fast combo. Ignore slow value engines — the long game is yours.
* **Hold {1}{G}{U} for Mutational Advantage** once the table names Mothman, and skip a creature to do it. Hexproof and indestructible on everything with counters, fizzling targeted exile, plus a proliferate. Make sure Mothman has a counter first.
* Heroic Intervention covers wipes; Ripples of Potential phases key pieces out.

## Mistakes this deck's pilots have made
* **Attacking with your only blocker.** Mothman has no vigilance. Below ~20 life, with an opponent showing a lethal swing, keep him home; never send him at a 40-life player when it empties your board.
* **Losing Hancock for good (seen once).** With a +1/+1 counter on him, undying won't return him. Keep him out of combat and away from first strikers.
* **Tapping Tato Farmer on an opponent's turn (seen once).** It untaps only on yours, so it can't block until then. A 6/9 blocker beats one extra land.
* **Irradiating yourself.** Mothman's enter and attack triggers, Contaminated Drink, Nuclear Fallout and Vault 12 all give you rad counters, and Winding Constrictor adds one more each time. Strong, the Brutish Thespian converts your radiation to life.
* **Killing your own board.** Nuclear Fallout and Finality hit everything; count survivors on your side first.
* **Milling yourself out.** Raul, Syr Konrad's activation and Mesmeric Orb mill you too.
